Here is another run of my Dossier - Earnings Call pipeline. This one looks at the Chewy's earnings call from yesterday. It is a multi-LLM summary and economic analysis with a closed-loop architecture. Each section and model output is fact checked against the actual earnings call transcript. Any discrepancy and it reruns, switches to new model, or fails. No lies, no hallucinations.

Another part of this product I wanted to highlight is the Business Principles section. This is where the multi-model LLM output is then compared to a very big knowledge base of economic and business theory and principles. I am using RAG here via FAISS. The earnings call summary and analysis is compared to decades of economic theory to make judgement calls on how the company is acting now.

This is the real power of Gen AI. We have the whole world at our fingertips that we can access in milliseconds. The applications are endless and immensely powerful.
