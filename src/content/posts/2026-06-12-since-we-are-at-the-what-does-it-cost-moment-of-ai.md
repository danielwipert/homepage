Since we are at the "What does it cost" moment of AI. I thought I would put together a quick and fun little menu....

The token market is all over the place. You can get your Michelin star models or your cheat eats. The biggest open question: What do you actaully need for your tasks and your business?

I am not at all convinced that the 5-star models are really the go-to for everyday business. After all, how many times do you get Cava vs. a $200 per plate sit-down meal? I think that applies very well to the Gen AI world and the hierarchy of models and cost.

Maybe let's stop drooling over the filet mignon and start thinking about what we are having for lunch on a Tuesday. Open weight, self-hosted models are basically like meal prep.
