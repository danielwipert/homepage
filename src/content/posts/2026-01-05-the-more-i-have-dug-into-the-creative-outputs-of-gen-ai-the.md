The more I have dug into the creative outputs of Gen AI, the less and less impressed I am with it. Gen AI has many layers. The most powerful layer in my mind is textual analysis and language generation. That alone is revolutionary.

Within the corporate creative fields, there has been a rush to embrace Gen AI. Sadly, this has led to really poor and embarrassing creative outputs. AI will no doubt change photo editing, graphic design, video production, music creation, etc. But for some reason, very large corporations have latched onto this idea that Gen AI replaces creatives and the creative process itself. This is textbook overpromising and underdelivering.

Look at the McDonalds AI ad as an example. It looks terrible, shockingly terrible for a multi-billion dollar company to release. It defines the term AI Slop, which many Fortune 100s are force-feeding us. Ironic for one of the founders of AI Slop to get upset about the term. One doth protest too much.

The weakness of Gen AI is quite apparent when you are an expert in the field it is trying to replicate. My deepest creative background is in songwriting and audio production. I have done it for 25 years now. It is not so much a hobby as it is an existential passion for me. Gen AI's attempts to create full songs are an abject failure, from an artistic perspective. Some Gen AI songs are close enough where the casual listener won't care. But in most creative fields, casual impersonation is never going to be good enough. AI Art is often dead art. It is lifeless without any creative spark. You need humans for that. Sad I know, they cost a lot!
