ADP National Employment Report - June 2025

ADP is saying job market is optimistic? Other reports say the opposite...

ADP Quote: "Our hiring and pay data are broadly indicative of a healthy economy. Employers have grown more optimistic that consumers, the backbone of the economy, will remain resilient."

What world are they living in?

The JOLTS report from yesterday tells the opposite story:

- Openings fell 275,000 to 7.44M, representing a 4.4% openings rate (near post-pandemic lows)
- Hires fell 261,000 to 5.204M, and the hiring rate dropped to 3.3%(also at one of the lowest levels since the pandemic)
- Demand weakening; employers cautious about expanding

So what gives?

ADP is saying “The labor market is cooling but healthy. Nothing alarming here.”

JOLTS has been saying consistently "Job openings are falling. Hiring is falling. Firms are not laying off workers, but they aren’t adding many either.”

But remember ADP is a look back report, JOLTS is a look forward report.

ADP is really saying "We paid 104K more people in July."

JOLTS is saying “Employers are posting fewer jobs and hiring more slowly. It's a no hire, fire job market."

What is actually happening?  
• Hiring continues, but it’s slower and concentrated in resilient sectors.  
• Labor demand (openings, growth plans) is cooling.  
• Employers are holding on to workers, but not aggressively expanding.
