Empire State Manufacturing — August 2025 (NY Fed)

Manufacturing rebounds on strong orders; margin squeeze and weak capex temper the outlook.

- General Business Conditions +6 pts to 11.9, highest since Nov 2024 (expansion)

- Demand: New orders +13.4 pts to 15.4; shipments steady at 12.2.

- Backlogs & inventories: Unfilled orders –5.5 (still shrinking). Inventories fell to –6.4 after a jump in July

- Supply chain: Delivery times up to 17.4 (meaning longer). Supply availability index –5.5 (negative = constrained).

- Labor: Employment +4.4 (slight hiring); workweek 0.2 (flat)

- Prices: Prices paid 54.1 (elevated); prices received 22.9 (moderate) = 31-pt gap = Margin Crush continues. Inflation is going up, it is gravity at this point.

Quality of growth: The orders jump alongside flat shipments and inventory drawdown suggests firms met demand by shipping from stock.

Bottlenecks creeping back: Longer delivery times and still-negative supply availability point to renewed friction upstream

Margins under pressure: Input costs remain steep while selling prices are only moderately higher; the large paid-minus-received gap implies continued margin compression unless firms gain pricing power.
