Customer Sentiment - Preliminary Results for August 2025 (Michigan)

Consumer sentiment dropped 5% in August, first decline in four months

- Main cause: rising inflation worries

- Buying conditions for durables fell 14%, lowest in a year due to high prices

- Current personal finances slipped slightly as purchasing power concerns grew

- Expected personal finances rose slightly; income expectations firmed but stayed low

- Consumers no longer expect the worst-case economic scenario feared in April (tariff concerns eased)

- Year-ahead inflation expectations rose from 4.5% to 4.9%

- Long-run inflation expectations rose from 3.4% to 3.9%

- Increases in both measures seen across all demographics and political groups

- Rise ends recent declines in inflation expectations, though still below April–May 2025 highs
