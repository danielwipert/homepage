Hot Off the Press -- SCE Credit Access - June 2025 Survey

What now? The SCE Credit Access Survey (part of the Survey of Consumer Expectations) is a quarterly survey conducted by the Federal Reserve Bank of New York. It tracks U.S. households’ experiences and expectations regarding credit and debt, providing insight into both the supply of and demand for credit from the consumer perspective.

Rejection Rates Are Rising  
• Credit application rejection rate increased from 8.9% → 9.7%  
• Suggests that lenders are becoming more selective, potentially tightening standards or reacting to credit risk concerns.  
• Particularly important if your business relies on consumer financing for big-ticket purchases.  
• But not in all cases. Auto Loan rejection rates dropped 52% compared to Feb 25 survey and Home Refi rejection rates dropped 65%.

🟢 Home Mortgages Application have risen 23% since the Feb 25 survey - Suggests people feel more secure in their finances, jobs, and creditworthiness.

Discouraged Borrowing Declined  
• Percent of consumers too discouraged to apply dropped from 8.6% → 7.2% (this is a 16% drop!)  
🟢 A positive sign: consumer confidence in applying for credit is improving.  
• May indicate greater optimism about creditworthiness, or adjusted expectations about rejection.

Rejection Expectations Have Improved  
•  Mortgage rejection expectations fell sharply: 47.6% → 40.9% (returning towards mean which sits at 37% over last 10 years)  
• Credit card rejection expectations down slightly: 32.3% → 31.9%  
• Auto loan rejection expectations down modestly: 33.5% → 30.6% ( modest but meaningful improvement in consumer sentiment)

Account Closures (by lenders) Rose  
• Lender-initiated closures jumped from 7.0% → 8.0%  
• 🚩 banks may be derisking portfolios or reducing consumer exposure

Emergency Fund Confidence Held Firm  
• 62.7% → 63.1% of respondents believe they could cover a $2,000 expense  
• This has remained depressed since Oct 2024 Survey. Previously 10 year average has been 68%
