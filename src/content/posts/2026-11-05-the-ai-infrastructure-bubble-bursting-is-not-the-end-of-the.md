The AI infrastructure bubble bursting is not the end of the world. It is a necessary correction to a market that became much too frothy, to the point of rabidity. The core friction point right now in the AI industry is the classic "Show-Me" problem. Everyone got super excited and jazzed up when ChatGPT demonstrated the truly insane potential of AI. But the reality of the transformation is taking a different shape than what has been assumed for the last 4 years.

The Show-Me problem is simple. People have made exuberant claims about what AI can and will do. As hundreds of billions poured into the market, people started to question what AI can actually do, right here, right now. The answer does not match the claims.

The AI stocks that are performing the worst are physical infrastructure. The pain is concentrated in the physical layer of the AI ecosystem because it is the most capital-intensive, slowest-moving, and most heavily leveraged part of the supply chain. It is also highly reactive to changes in AI usage. Think of it in these terms: according to 2026 Gartner projections, worldwide AI infrastructure spending is forecasted to hit nearly $1.48 trillion this year, while AI software spending is expected to reach only $461 billion. Something doesn't add up.

The market rushed to fund these delusional build-outs based on the potential of AI usage to skyrocket exponentially and indefinitely. The same exact thing happened with fiber cable during dot com boom. They built a network of cable to support internet usage that wouldn't exist for years. They were making a big bet on a specific rate of growth, and they got it wrong. Now it is data centers, GPUs, and cooling systems. Same issues, different tech.

While the headline story of this crash will be about regulatory fears stemming from the Dems taking over, my read is different. The bubble was always going to pop because the supply was so far outstripping demand; gravity would kick in eventually. Now it has.

The election shock was just the pin that pressed on the most distended part of the bubble: CoreWeave, Vistra, Hut 8, Cipher Mining. Demand was never going to keep up with this manic drive to build supply. This is just the AI market maturing, not collapsing.
