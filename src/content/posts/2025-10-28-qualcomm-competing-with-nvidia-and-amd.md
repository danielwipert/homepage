"Qualcomm: Competing with Nvidia and AMD"

I just finished the phenomenal book on the beginning of Nvidia and how they won the AI race: The Thinking Machine by Stephen Witt. I have to say after reading that book, I find it hard to believe that any company is going to touch Nvidia until 2030 at the earliest. They created the entire field of modern AI computing. Huang created it out of thin air and spent decades working on it when everyone told him he was crazy. You can't play catch up with that type of innovation.

- Qualcomm company unveiled its first data center accelerators, the AI200 and AI250, set for launch in 2026 and 2027. These liquid-cooled chips are built on Qualcomm’s Hexagon neural processing units, long used in its smartphones but now scaled for industrial AI infrastructure. The stock jumped nearly 9% on the news.

- Unlike Nvidia, Qualcomm is targeting the inference phase of AI (where trained models are executed) promising “better energy cost-efficiency than its competitors.” Each rack consumes about 160 kilowatts, similar to Nvidia’s systems, but with lower operating costs.

- Qualcomm’s modular approach will let customers buy full systems or individual components, a move that could attract cloud giants like Amazon, Microsoft, and Google, all seeking to diversify suppliers.
