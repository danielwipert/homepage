Wow! Those new U of Michigan customer sentiment numbers are something else. But they always bury the lede, the political tables were updated today...

2025 continues to be the story of two realities for GOP and Dems voters. Just looking at my favorite metric from the survey, Index of Customer Expectations aka "what does the economic future look like to you?"

- Dems are apoplectic again at 29 - The Historical Dem Avg is 72 going back to 1980.
- GOP is in La La Land, but their delusional world seems to be popping. Their future index dropped to 96 from 100. The historical average for them is 78. So still overly optimistic but starting to shift down to earth again.
- The big change has been Independents. They are back down to 43 after stabilizing in the 50s during the summer. Historical ave is 69.

It's a lot of bad news. That's what people are feeling and saying. Dems are at historic lows, so are Indys. GOP is still flying high, but they are running out of fuel. The shutdown is absolutely having a big effect on these numbers.
