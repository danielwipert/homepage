The White House put out some ridiculous Food Inflation lies yesterday. Here is the reality...

This is what the White House said yesterday in an official press release:

"Over the past year, prices actually declined for cheese, cereals & bars, fresh fruits, frozen meals, packaged meals, condiments, batteries, computers & accessories, phones & accessories, medicines, oral care, vitamins & supplements, outdoor products, and toys & games."

The US Government measures food costs and inflation. They have since 1947. They release the data every month. It is free for everyone to see and review. Here is the truth:

Food prices, based on the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers: Food (CPIUFDSL), have increased by about 1.9% so far in 2025

- If a gallon of milk cost $4.00 at the start of the year, it would now cost roughly $4.08.
- A dozen eggs priced at $4.95 in January would cost about $5.04 in September
- A loaf of bread that was $2.50 would now be around $2.55.

No, food prices are not going down. They are going up. It looks like they will surpass an increase of 2% for the year, which is higher than the Fed inflation target.
