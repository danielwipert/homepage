I was playing around with the Atlanta Fed Market Distro Spider Chart. I can't pretend to understand all the ins and outs of it. But I did spot some interesting data points, specifically the change in Hiring rates among employers and Quitting rates among employees over the last 2 years. Pretty stark change. (pink lines I added below).

It looks like this pattern has been in place for the last couple of years. Quit Rate peaked in Feb 2023, but hiring hasn't really rebounded since Feb 2022. An interesting lag, people continue to quit their job as hiring tapered off.
