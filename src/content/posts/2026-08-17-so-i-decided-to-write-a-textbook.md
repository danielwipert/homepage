So I decided to write a textbook. I have been reading, researching, and writing so much about AI that I figured I might as well organize my work into a final product.

While I am obsessed with the technical minutiae of Generative AI, I must admit that my wealth of experience is actually in real world business operations. I dare to say I am an expert in how to build and run a business. I have spent the last 15 years in the trenches of startups. It is with that deep knowledge and experience that I am approaching AI. My biggest value add is in helping others integrate Gen AI into actual work, not just the pie in sky promises coming from Silicon Valley. That is why I decided to write this iterative textbook, AI Operations Management.

The gap between the AI nerds and the normal business world is pretty staggering right now. The distance between tech and business ops has always been wide, but now it is hard for each side to see each other. Even bracketing out the extreme distain for AI by the general public, it is alarming to me how little the average business operator knows or understands about deep learning and LLMs. I am sure it is akin to how business transitioned from analog to digital, but I think this is happening much faster, and the transition is much more severe.

As someone who has been in the details of building business processes for the last 15 years, I can tell you with high confidence that no one really has any idea what they are doing. I have worked in tech startups where most people didn't even understand the basics of front-end vs. back-end or how cloud computing actually works. And you know what, these businesses functioned and even flourished. Not everyone needs to be a CS PhD. But at the same time, not having a core understanding of technology can be destabilizing and disabling.

AI Operations Management is my attempt to help educate people by establishing a new discourse. This is not AIOPs, which is a niche technical field within IT. AI Operations Management (AIOM) is how you manage deployed AI in a real-world business. How you determine what tools to use or build, and how you manage the actual impact they have on the business. That's it.

But the "it" here is a massive black hole right for most businesses, whether a 3-person startup or a Fortune 50 corporation. It is total chaos out there with tokenmaxxing, hallucinated legal briefs, and production databases being deleted. There is no map, no plan, no way to see where to go. This book is an attempt to provide at least one clear direction to go in.

The book will be written in stages, chapter by chapter. It will always be a WIP. I will go back and edit old chapters if new information comes to light. This is not a dusty old textbook you will find in the stacks. It is a living document that will change as the world changes.
