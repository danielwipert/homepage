CNN Headline Today: "The US economy rebounded sharply in the second quarter"

Let's go beyond the headline here to see the larger truth...

CNN’s headline is technically true: Real GDP grew +3.0% in Q2 2025, a big swing from the –0.5% decline in Q1. The details tell a MUCH more complicated story.

Why the optimism makes sense:  
• After a sluggish Q1, this rebound signals that a recession is not inevitable.  
• Consumer spending stabilized, and inflation cooled (core PCE down to 2.5%)  
• The headline number gives the Fed breathing room, no emergency cuts needed.

Where the veneer wears off the optimism:  
• Nearly the entire GDP boost came from a sharp drop in imports (which mechanically lifts GDP). That’s not a sign of strength, it’s an accounting fluke. Imported Goods were down 35% from Q1.  
• GDP includes changes in inventories and trade flows which can swing dramatically quarter to quarter, but don’t reflect domestic demand. FSPDP does. Final sales to private domestic purchasers (FSPDP), arguably the best measure of true demand, rose just 1.2%, the weakest since Q4 2022.  
 • Business investment slowed, and inventories were a drag, not what you'd expect in a "strong" economy. Investment in commercial structures dropped 10% from Q1.

Yes, Q2 was a rebound, but it was import-led, not demand-driven. If you strip away trade noise, the U.S. economy is still growing, but not booming. Strong GDP with weak FSPDP means we are coasting and our engine is puttering.

Don't blindly believe the headlines
