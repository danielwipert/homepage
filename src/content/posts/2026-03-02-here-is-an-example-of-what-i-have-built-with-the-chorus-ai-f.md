Here is an example of what I have built with the Chorus AI framework... This is Dossier: a seven-stage, multi-model pipeline that takes a document and produces an analytical intelligence product that is grounded, traceable, and defensible. Not a summary. Not a chatbot response. A structured output you can actually stake a decision on. True intelligence, not fabricated confidence you get from ChatGPT.

This dossier is for The Economist article "America’s trade chaos is just beginning" The dossier summarizes the article from multiple perspectives, and then gives you broader context on the topic and how the article fits within the larger discourse.

Notice that one summarizer model failed because it fabricated points in its summary. Check out the log on the last page. Hallucinations are failed out of the system. If it doesn't pass the strict fact check, it is removed.

7 different models touched this document. Multi-model systems are closer to real intelligence, not single monolithic models. Science runs on peer-reviewed papers. Not a single perspective. Truth is the collective discourse, not the solo thinker.

This same pipeline can be used for any text document. I intend to build specific products for use cases like: contract reviews, legal briefs, and academic articles, all with specific pipelines and modules to complete the task with multi-models, true verification, and a closed-loop, always.
