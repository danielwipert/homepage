Jev has broken into the top 10 models on Open Router today. Look out, folks. I have loved what I have seen and built so far with Jev. Yes, it is a simple concept. But it is a simple concept packaged in the right way.

You think McDonald's invented the cheeseburger? AI is still in its infancy; the first marketing wave hasn't even crested yet. We are going to see a lot more model distillations and refinements packaged into products.

What I would love to see next is a company that builds a very complex AI system with closed feedback loops and 50 cheap LLMs into a single pipeline. When I think of an actual AI agent, I see hundreds of nodes interacting with each other, everything gated and checked, and tons of feedback loops.

The term Compound AI Systems has popped up to refer to something like this, a large network of nodes interacting in a single system. Jev fits right into that thesis. It is no longer about one model solving all problems. I think we are shifting towards a much more refined version of AI systems. What I see is a lot of LLMs and SMLs in a single system interacting with each other. Not a swarm. Rather, a flock, where everything is coordinated. Or maybe even like a mechanical clock, each gear has its own purpose.
