The new issue of Closing the Loop is up. I went through a few drafts on this one. I ended up combining a couple issues into one. I think the biggest problem is that tool usage within AI is all over the place. People are having a hard enough time wrapping their heads around the different platforms and models. Tool connectivity and usage are just too overwhelming.

My focus is here is really on Access Control. AI is so powerful that you really need to slow down when it comes to integrating any AI into your business systems. Read-only is the way to go until you have a very strong guardrail harness set up. Giving write access to AI is pretty crazy unless you have a very good understanding of AI safety and governance.

I know this has been all over the news the last few weeks, but AI safety is absolutely non-negotiable. No serious business would ever implement AI without dogmatic guardrails, and backstops all over their system. In the rush to adopt AI, most companies took the exact wrong approach.
