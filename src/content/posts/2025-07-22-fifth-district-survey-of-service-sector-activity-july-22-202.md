Fifth District Survey of Service Sector Activity - July 22, 2025

Service Sector Stabilizes, But Hiring and Confidence Lag Behind -- The service sector showed slight improvement in July, with modest gains in revenue and demand. Business conditions remain weak, and hiring has stalled as firms continue to be cautious. Wages continue to rise and are expected to keep climbing. Input cost increases are lessening, but price paid by customers is not keeping up. Expect increase in customer price in future surveys.

Fifth Federal Reserve District: DC, MD, NC, SC, VA, and most of WV

What’s improving?  
• Sales and demand are back in positive territory, a welcome turnaround from last month’s dip. Businesses are cautiously optimistic that customer activity will keep growing over the next six months.  
• Companies expect to raise prices slightly more in the coming year, signaling confidence in customer willingness to pay. Cost are expected to come down as well.

Where’s the caution?  
• Business conditions have improved slightly but are still quite negative. They have not been positive since Jan 2025 survey.  
• Capital Expenditures continue to be depressed, stuck in negative territory since Feb 2025.  
• Hiring has stalled: employment levels were flat in July, and plans to add staff in the months ahead have been pulled back.  
  • But wages are still going up, probably to keep talent, and wages are expected to rise, 44% of companies expect to pay their people more in 6 months.
