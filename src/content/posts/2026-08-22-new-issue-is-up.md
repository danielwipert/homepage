New issue is up! I think most people have a general understanding of how to prompt engineer and maybe even a basic concept of how to use RAG. But fine-tuning is where the drop-off is massive for business operators.

It is important for everyone to know these three types of model interactions, because you will be asked to have a perspective and strategy on how to use each in your role.

In short, prompt away. RAG if you must, but better to get an expert to do it. Fine-tuning, don't touch it unless you understand the actual math behind LLMs.
