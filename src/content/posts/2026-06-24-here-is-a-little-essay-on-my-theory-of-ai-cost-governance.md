Here is a little essay on my theory of AI Cost Governance. I think this is really the missing piece of the puzzle in 2026. Once we start really working on it, I think Gen AI will take off to its next era, which will be smaller models being asked to do smaller tasks, all part of a massive modular compound AI system. That is where true Agentic AI begins.
