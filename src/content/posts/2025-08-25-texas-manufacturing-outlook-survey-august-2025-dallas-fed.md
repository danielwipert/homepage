Texas Manufacturing Outlook Survey - August 2025 (Dallas Fed)

I am looking closely at the Prices Paid vs Prices Received gap in all these fed reports. I am fascinating by this gap and what it means for inflation.

The latest info from Dallas mirrors what we saw on Friday from Philly. The companies surveyed are telling us they are paying more to create their products than what they are getting from customers. Costs are increasing much more than prices.

While this is not direct inflationary pressure per se, it is definitely cost pressure which creates a margin crush. Companies are telling us across the aboard, our costs are increasing but we are not raising our prices... yet.

The pattern in these fed reports demonstrates persistent broad-based upstream cost pressure. The cost pressure and gap is not inflation itself, just risk, but that risk is increasing and sustained. They key will be demand, Philly was soft, Dallas showed recovery. If demand grows, inflation is a forgone conclusion. Something to watch as PCE / Core PCE drops this Friday.
