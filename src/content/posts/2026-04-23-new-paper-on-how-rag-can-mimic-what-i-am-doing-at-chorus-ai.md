New Paper on how RAG can mimic what I am doing at Chorus AI Systems...

RAG is one of the most powerful and weak aspects of the AI ecosystem. If you don't know what it is, definitely read up on the basics of RAG and the concepts. It is one of the vital organs of AI systems.

RAG very often relies on very old school math and vector databases. Nothing wrong with that, it is clearly working super well for AI systems now. But RAG is just V1. We need V2 , and then keeping evolving it. LLMs evolution has far outpaced RAG evolution.

This paper is a good addition to that RAG evolution discourse. It argues that RAG is too noisy: too reductive, one-shot across multiple data sources that can not really capture the required data. The end results is RAG data and text retrieval is incomplete, not fully contextualized, which can be misleading.

Namely, a single LLM RAG can not properly contextualize complex and large data sets. You need multiple models to make multiple RAG calls, and then synthesize the data. This is what I have already built at Chorus AI Systems.

A system that uses multiple models to do multiple RAG calls, then synthesizes that data is much better than a one-shot, one-model RAG call. This paper argues that their Multi-agent RAG with synthesis (MASS-RAG) is superior to single-agent RAG.

I am in totally agreeance with this paper, and I have been building out this idea. I have built Dossier, Dossier - Earnings Call, and now DAM (Data Analytics Manager) based on a similar theory to this paper.

Truth in AI is never one-shot or one-model. It comes from a conversation of models. If we want to trust AI, we need to model it after human knowledge, which is always a discourse and always evolving. Truth and trust are not destinations but they are ways of building, creating, and thinking.
