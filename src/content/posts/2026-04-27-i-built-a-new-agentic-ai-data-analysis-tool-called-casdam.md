I built a new Agentic AI data analysis tool called CASDAM. I really wanted to push forward Gen AI into Trusted AI, and this is how I did it...

One of the most fascinating aspects of the Gen AI revolution is how quickly people have assumed it will take over entire work roles. But at the same time, Gen AI has proven to be inherently unstable and untrustworthy. This is opposite of what we want.

I started to wonder, how can we bridge this massive gap in the market between what Gen AI claims it can do and what it can actually do. The major AI companies are flying in the wrong direction. They are trying to make models bigger and smarter to claim some fictional and delusional status of Artificial General Intelligence. This all seems very utopianist and absurd to me. LLMs are not smart, they don't even reason or understand. But the LLMs that drive Generative AI are insanely powerful. How can we harness that power while reducing the high risk that comes with it?

CASDAM, or Chorus AI Systems Data Analytics Manager, is my attempt at harnessing the true power of Gen AI while also making it production ready for actual work environments. A real world tool that will increase productivity dramatically while maintaining a high level of trust. The reality is that CASDAM is less a tool or product, a more a framework for Trusted Agenetic AI.

Here are the basic tenets:

- AI should check its own work - Instead of one model giving you an answer, the system is built in gated layers. Each step validates the one before it. If something looks wrong, it doesn’t move forward.

- Every insight must be backed by real data - The system creates a fixed set of facts first. Every conclusion has to point back to those facts. If it can’t, it gets removed.

- Different models keep each other honest - We don’t rely on one “smart” model. We use multiple models with different strengths and let them cross-check each other. That reduces blind spots.

We’re moving from a world where AI generates content to one where it supports real business decisions. That shift demands a different architecture entirely: one built on verification, structure, and accountability, not just output.

CASDAM is an early version of that future: where intelligence isn’t just produced, it’s governed, tested, and trusted. That’s the difference between something interesting and something that can actually run your business.
