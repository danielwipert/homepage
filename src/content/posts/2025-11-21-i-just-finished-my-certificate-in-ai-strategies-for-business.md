I just finished my certificate in AI Strategies for Business Transformation from Northwestern University - Kellogg School of Management. I wanted to pass along some thoughts on the program and what I learned.

First of all, I am very glad I undertook this program at Kellogg. It was eye opening. We had students and professors from all over the world, who are all trying to tackle the complex problem of AI implementation in real world business. The intense acceleration of AI's evolution makes teaching classes on this subject nearly impossible, but the Kellogg team did a great job.

The core lesson I learned in this program is that AI has already remade the business world. Various forms of AI have been around for decades, but the leap to Generative AI and now Agentic AI is just massive. I came into the course thinking huge changes were coming. I left knowing the change has already happened, and even bigger waves of change will happen over and over for the next decade. We are entering an unprecedented era of economic evolution. AI will rival the changes from horse to automobile, from gas lamp to electricity.

At the same time, most businesses are just not ready for the change that is happening. The two main issues I am seeing with AI implementations now are data hygiene and a fundamental misunderstanding of how Generative AI actually works.

Data Hygiene - This has been a widespread problem since I started in the startup retail space. For whatever reason, businesses outside of pure tech simply do not want to acknowledge or accept they must now be a tech company AND be a clothing company or beauty company or shoe company. You can't just be a retail company anymore. Those days ended about 12 years ago. You need to be tech first, which means understanding and respecting data. AI simply can not function within a company on a simple or complex level without pristine data and data management. This is especially true for Gen AI, which will take off in any direction your data points. You better have a good map!

What is Gen AI - I understand we had a Copernican Revolution in AI in the last three years, and the pace of change is disorienting. But you should not be speaking about AI or heaven forbid trying to implement new AI solutions at your company without understanding the basics: machine learning to deep learning with neural networks to large language models with transformers. It takes an hour to learn this, tops. The reason this is so important is that without a general understanding of how Generative AI actually functions, you can not safety integrate AI into your business. The biggest misunderstanding is that Gen AI is a probability based technology, which means hallucinations are a feature, not a bug. The more you know!

I will close with this. Buckle up. No one alive has seen a technological change like this. No one has ever seen it happen this fast. We are living through an unimaginable moment in human history, for better or worse.
