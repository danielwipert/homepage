AI Systems are failing as AI models are flourishing...

Covering AI news is like trying to catch raindrops, there are too many stories coming at us way too quickly. What rises to the top are the sensational stories. A deeper and broader truth lies hidden just beneath the service. We are hearing every day now that a new Gen AI model has reach a new high score in a benchmark. Superintelligence and AGI must me around the corner, right?

No, I don't think so. We are in a massive groupthink situation where billions of dollars of investment are hiding the fuller reality of AI. Yeah its a bubble, but not just a financial one. As this AI model arms race heats up, we are seeing small little stories about how AI is failing. Here is a detailed one from last week where Claude Code deleted production databases along with all the backups https://lnkd.in/gUhX_Fry

That story popped for a day, then it was gone. Instead we are inundated with stories about how AI is becoming smarter, how AI is going to put 50% of white collar works out on the streets,  and how AI will become a literal weapon. If it bleeds it leads. What I am not reading or seeing is how AI is actually going to function in a real-world work environment outside of assisting a programmer or helping someone who is already very technically inclined. Or how AI will help run a business or any of the millions of Critical Systems we use to run our society. There is a reason we hear the utopian blatherings of Sam Altman and not how say Gen AI will run a power grid.

Because Gen AI is not viable for Critical Systems. It certainly can be, and one day will be a part of these integral systems. But right now, Silicon Valley is overly obsessed with pushing model evolution towards terminal velocity and spewing worthless generalizations about how nebulous AI agents will take over all knowledge work. What we are missing is a clear roadmap for how Gen AI will actually integrate with the complex and vital systems that run our lives.

We must ask ourselves: What great breakthrough has Gen AI made when it comes to systems theory, control theory, and modern cybernetics? I think the reality is that while Gen AI is a great leap forward, it has not rewritten any of the basic principles or rules that govern cybernetics and modern systems theory, which underpin almost every single system we use in our daily lives. This is why AI systems are failing, because they are not actual systems. They are out-of-control controllers, running amuck.

The power of Gen AI is like going from a horse to a jet engine. Yet we are still strapping that jet engine to a horse carriage. All of our critical and not so critical systems will need to be reimagined and rebuilt to incorporate the immense power of AI. That will take many years to do. Only then will Gen AI reach its full potential.
