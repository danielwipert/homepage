For people not in the apparel sourcing world, it is hard to understand how devastating the tariff announcement was yesterday. For the last year, companies all over the world have been trying to navigate a potential Trump presidency. This is on all sides: factories, brands, logistics providers. The collective assumption was that China was going to get hit hard again if Trump won. So the logical reaction was to shift apparel production to other countries with similar or lower cost footprints: Vietnam, Cambodia, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh.

The tariffs announced yesterday against these countries will totally upend the entire apparel industry worldwide. It will pretty much hurt all countries, especially the United States. A fact I love to throw out to people. In 1990, at least 50% of clothing bought by Americans was made in the USA. Now it is below 2%. The world has changed, and it is not going back. With or without us, the world will move forward.
