CoreWeave stock is tanking now, and there is a hushed murmur... is this it? Is this the dreaded AI Bubble about to burst? CoreWeave is kind of the canary in the coalmine. They are IaaS, or Infrastructure as Service, aka Neo Cloud. If the bubble was becoming distended, we would see it in the Neo Cloud companies first.

Taking a wider viewpoint, about one trillion dollars has been invested into the AI infrastructure layer: data centers, power grids, and specialized hardware. The idea here is eerily similar to what happened during the dot com boom. The belief then was that the internet would be so popular that demand would grow exponentially for many years.

During the late 1990s, telecom companies (like WorldCom) took on tens of billions of dollars in high-yield debt to lay millions of miles of fiber cables, assuming that internet traffic growth would immediately absorb any amount of bandwidth supplied. When demand took longer to monetize than expected, unit prices for bandwidth collapsed, leading to a massive wave of corporate defaults, even though the internet itself ultimately fulfilled its promise years later.

Investors are starting to call *ullshit on the one trillion dollar layout for AI. One of the big reasons is what I have been talking about for a good year now. The big spend on AI presupposed that most of the usage and focus would be on cutting-edge frontier models like Fable and Astra. But via Applied AI, we have come to learn that smaller models and open-weight models are good enough for most business automations. In other words, if a Toyota Camry gets you from point A to B, why would you buy a BMW?

CoreWeave is stuck in the middle of this. To buy all those expensive NVIDIA GPUs, CoreWeave borrowed tens of billions of dollars. Even if business is good, they have to make massive monthly debt payments to the banks. Investors are getting nervous that if renting out those computers slows down even a little bit, CoreWeave won't have enough cash coming in to pay back what it owes.

The other problem is the robbing Peter to pay Paul shendangians going on in AI. A lot of the money floating around in the AI world has been moving in a circle: big tech companies and investors lend money to AI startups, and those startups then use that same borrowed cash to rent computers from companies like CoreWeave. Financial regulators and Wall Street are starting to call this out as a "financing loop" or a potential bubble. If investors stop pouring cash into those AI startups, those startups stop renting computers from CoreWeave, leaving CoreWeave stuck with giant bills and empty supercomputers.

Spurred by high-yield debt and $39 billion in planned CapEx, CoreWeave faces mounting credit default risks as enterprise customers shift toward cheaper, open-weight models to curb AI compute spend. We are witnessing the classic "infrastructure overbuild" phase of a bubble, and it will pop eventually. Just a matter of when.
