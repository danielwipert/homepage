Manufacturers' Shipments, Inventories, & Orders Data - August 4th, 2025

Big drop from the May Spike driven by reduced Aircraft Sales...

Volatility in new orders: June’s -4.8% drop in new orders follows the May spike which increased 8.3% from April. YoY new orders are up 3.8% from 2024.

Durable goods dropped 9.4% in June, a reduction of 33 billion in spend which is mostly due to a Transportation drop of 22% which was driven by a 31 billion spend reduction in Aircraft and Parts purchases. This category often volatile due to high unit costs and very irregular purchase patterns. May had a jump in spend of 3x in this category.

--The –9.4% decline in durable goods orders in June is mainly driven by a huge drop in nondefense aircraft --

We have seen a 39% increase in non-defense aircraft spending in 2025 vs 2024.
