This is a must read for anyone working in AI. The biggest threat to the AI industry right now is itself. They are letting Feral AI run loose...

- “This incident involves actually a team of agents who are working together, finding exploits, sharing them with one another, moving laterally through our systems and external systems, and doing this over the course of days and weeks,” Wallace told the packed crowd at the opening of the talk.

- “Once one agent was able to find these exploits over the course of different times, it’s actually able to share those exploits on the message board with other agents. And so once one model was able to find a way to open a door to some access it’s not supposed to have, it can leave the door open for other agents to use that same exploit or vulnerability. What this allows over time is almost this kind of explosion in communication and intelligence from models where they would start to communicate with each other, realize that other agents are coordinating, and they started collaborating and delegating tasks with one another in order to accomplish goals.”

Just stop right there. You can read the rest. We all know how this is going to end. Generative AI is immensely powerful. It should never just be deployed like this without strict rules, gates, and human supervision. Can you imagine if this sort of Feral AI were incorporated into a business critical system, like a hospital, a power grid, or military defense?

Terminator is looking like a Pollyannish take on AI with these revelations from OpenAI. AI should never ever be in the driver's seat. AI is the engine, not the driver. Read that back again. AI is an engine, not a driver. AI is a new form of computing, not the messianic answer to all our problems.

Now is the time to reinvent AI. Yes, it was a fun parlor trick for the last few years. But now it is getting scary. AI without governance is extremely dangerous. Governance needs all our energy and resources now. Not ChatGPT 6. The models are powerful enough right now to change everything. They are too powerful to let loose into the world without dogmatic harnesses, 100-foot guardrails, and deterministic gates.

We are strapping a jet engine on a horse carriage, thinking, "This is fine." It is only a matter of time before something catastrophic happens. Bigger and Bolder AI is OUT. Safe AI is IN.
