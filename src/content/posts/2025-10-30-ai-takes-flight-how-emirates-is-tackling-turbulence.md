AI Takes Flight: How Emirates Is Tackling Turbulence

“Pilots now rely on live weather intelligence and AI-powered forecasts.”

It is crazy to see how quickly AI has been integrated throughout different industries. It is really leap forward for computing and augmenting human input.

- Emirates is using AI, predictive analytics, and real-time global weather data to make air travel smoother

- By integrating machine learning and pilot-fed turbulence reports, Emirates can now predict and avoid unstable air in real time, improving comfort and safety across its global routes.

- This has already led to a measurable decline in severe turbulence incidents.

“A comprehensive, data-driven strategy has already led to a measurable decline in unexpected severe turbulence across its global network.”
