Continuing my discussion of AI Value, what does it actually add? I have talked about software development, cx, and hr. Now let's talk c-suite and mgmt strategy level.

To recap, AI adds a ton of productivity to software development. We are talking about 10x code production, but only a minimal rise in releases. Why? Human code review. AI can't replace it right now because it is not good enough or trustworthy enough. Also, maybe we don't want AI to be checking AI?

In sales and cx, we do see a rise in value with AI, but mostly in the gaps of the sales and cx funnels. If there was no service or touchpoint, AI can add one and increase revenue for a company. Outside of that, not seeing a hugely significant value add with AI right now.

In HR, there is definitely value add for AI. Now that is probably a pretty controversial statement, but a big study does show an uptick: Applicants interviewed by AI were 12% more likely to get an offer and about 18% more likely to start the job.

The bottom line is that AI is adding value, but not uniformly across different verticals and nowhere near the break-even point. From an executive viewpoint, the picture kinda bleak:

- In a survey of about 750 executives, mostly chief financial officers, run in late 2025 and early 2026, firms attributed a 1.8% gain in labor productivity to AI in 2025 and expected 3.0% in 2026 - https://lnkd.in/gFVagbjx

- A second executive survey is more sober still. Firms reported that AI had raised their productivity by about 0.29% over the previous three years, and expected 1.4% over the next three - https://lnkd.in/g-XBKmXb

The answer to the AI value question is actually clear. It adds the most when it is directly interacting with customers or providing solid support via data, analysis, or assistance. Outside of that, it is often just piling more and more on people's plates.

Put AI where it touches customers or sharpens decisions, and measure revenue, conversion, or outcomes... not just activity. Where AI feeds a review step, spend on review capacity before spending on more generation. Do not trust how fast AI feels. In every study that measured both, felt gains ran far, far ahead of measured ones.
