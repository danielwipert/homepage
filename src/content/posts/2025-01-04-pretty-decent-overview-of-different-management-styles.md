Pretty decent overview of different management styles. The key is at the end. All good mangers code switch styles for the situation and people they are managing. It’s not EQ, it’s emotional improv.

The best managers I know do a few things:

- They treat everyone as a human being first, not some line on a budget spreadsheet.

- They lead by consensus not domination

- They act decisively with limited information and completely own their decision.

- They never blame a subordinate for a failure. Being a leader means taking responsibility, no matter what. The buck stops with you.
