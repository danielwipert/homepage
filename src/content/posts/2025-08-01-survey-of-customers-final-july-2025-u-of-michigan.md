Survey of Customers - Final July 2025 (U of Michigan)

Slight MoM uptick, but Customer Sentiment is at Historic Lows...

"﻿Consumer sentiment improved for the second straight month, inching up a scant single index point from June. Current conditions rose about 5% to its highest reading since February 2025, while the expectations index fell slightly." -  Director Joanne Hsu

--"A rise in sentiment among stockholders was partially offset by a decline among consumers who do not own stocks."

--"Consumers are hardly optimistic about the trajectory of the economy, even as their worries have softened since April 2025."

This is perhaps a bold understatement. While Customer Sentiment has ticked up from June a single point, the overall mood from customers is extremely gloomy.

Look at my historical chart to see where we stand now. The 10 year average is a index of 82. We are 20 ticks off that.

The 3 month trailing is 58, the 12 month training is 65

What we are seeing is an extended depressed customer sentiment. We have not seen good numbers here since the summer 2021, and we haven't seen much optimism since the month Covid broke out in Feb 2020.

Also to note, we have not seen this level of depressed sentiment in a very long time. This is worse than even the depths of the Great Recession. We hit a 3 month trailing of 58 in December 2008. We have broken that barrier post-covid FIVE times. This month we have equaled that low point again.
