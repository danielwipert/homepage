I finally finished this certification for Machine Learning: Linear Algebra from Imperial College London, and I must say it was no walk in the park. I wanted to push and test myself, and this certification definitely did that.

It felt like trudging through a dense swamp while also climbing a steep mountain. Near the end, it really was just one foot in front of the other. What did I learn? A ton about math that I hadn't touched since college. Most of which I had never seen before. What can I say now about ML and AI that I couldn't before? Leave most of it to the experts.

If you are working in business today facing the AI question constantly, you do not need to drop anything and take a course like this. I did, because I am eccentric and like to punish myself a bit. But I would not recommend it to anyone other than people that are very passionate about getting under the hood of AI. That is not most people.

What I will say is this: you should at least have a general understanding of how LLMs work. That does take a little bit of linear algebra to achieve. Not much, just tip your toe and see how it feels. If you like it, jump in with the rest of us!
