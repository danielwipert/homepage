As a current job searcher myself, it feels different out there than in the past five years. I graduated from grad school in spring 2008, and I struggled to find anything that year. It was brutal. This feels a bit similar now. The hard data doesn't lie either. Job openings have been consistently dropping since Summer of 2022. So yes tariffs, but the job market had already been tightening. As I learned in 2008, you have to be dynamic, strong, and flexible to make it work. This time will be the same.
