Philly Fed, March Manufacturing Survey: "Over 53 percent of the firms reported labor supply as at least a slight constraint to capacity utilization in the current quarter, and 47 percent of the firms cited supply chains as a constraint. Additionally, 79 percent of the firms indicated that uncertainty was at least a slight constraint this quarter, with 30 percent categorizing the impact as moderate and 24 percent categorizing it as significant. Looking ahead over the next three months, most firms expect the impacts of various factors to stay the same. However, 64 percent of the firms expect the impact of uncertainty to worsen over the next three months, and 44 percent expect supply chain impacts to worsen."
