I have been working on this one for a while. It is clear that AI will go through a massive transformation over the next few years. Sadly, the powerful technological advancement of Generative AI has been co-opted by many of the same people that helped create it to sell both a fairy tale and a nightmare. All of it, a work of fiction.

No, AI is not going to take everyone's job. Corporate leaders swallowed that lie hook, line, and sinker. You could write a couple of books about why. On the other side are charlatans like Ed Zitron, who quack all over CNBC about how all of AI is a pyramid scheme. Whether it's Jenson's "End of Coding" or Altman's "One-Person Billion-Dollar Company," Gen AI has inspired many fantastical stories, most without a whiff of truth to them.

What I do know is this. Generative AI is absolutely a transformative technology on par with the personal computer and the internet. The problem is we don't know how it will be transformative. People have used that gap in understanding to stuff their pockets, stroke their ego, and dismantle companies for their own personal gain.

Gen AI is a new form of computing. It needs new rules, new disciplines, and new ways of thinking. I think approaching AI like it is software is a mistake.
