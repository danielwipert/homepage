Artificial intelligence: Google unveils plan to restart US nuclear plant

The new arms race in AI is focused on power consumption. But everything I have seen is that a normal Gen AI query is similar in power cost to an old school google query. I think the issue is less the efficiency of Gen AI, but rather the explosive increase in usage that is coming. Gen AI is a great leap forward, and it will touch pretty much every part of our daily life. We do not have the power structure in USA to support that, so we will see a lot more nuclear power here for better or worse.

- “As the U.S. enters a new era of innovation and opportunity driven by AI, this strategic collaboration aims to enable Google to grow its business needs responsibly" - Google

- The International Energy Agency projects that data center electricity use will more than double by 2030. Google has already announced partnerships to develop three new advanced nuclear plants in the U.S., while Microsoft is backing the restart of the Three Mile Island plant for its own AI workloads.
