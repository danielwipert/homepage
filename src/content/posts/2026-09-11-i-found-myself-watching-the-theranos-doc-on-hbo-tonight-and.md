I found myself watching the Theranos doc on HBO tonight, and I was struck by this passage in it...

Silicon Valley is really good at making a web-based email client or really great at making a chat app with emojis on it. But Sillcon Valley is trying to do things where people's lives are on the line. We're trying to make autonomous vehicles. We're trying to make medical devices. We're trying to do these things that if you don't do it right, quite frankly, people can die. You need to approach it with a different lens. The problem is, when you are developing the future, you don't know what the impact is going to be, because no one has seen this before. And the testing ground is often just the general public.

What Elizabeth Holmes did was so immoral and vile that it is hard to fathom. She lied for decades, committed incalculable acts of fraud, and directly hurt people that she was supposed to be helping. But I am struck more by this quote as it relates to AI, right now.

The Anthropic whistleblower said this week, "The people building AI earnestly believe that it could kill us all by the end of the decade." The intense arms race between OpenAI, Anthropic, and the Chinese labs has become an existential threat to humanity. Now, I am not some anti-AI lemming. I don't think the threat is that AI has become so powerful that it begins to take over. Rather, I think the real threat is the greed and hubris of people working at the frontier labs. They are releasing Feral AI into real-world systems that will have real-world consequences for all of us.

Just to name a few real-world consequences. Several lawsuits were filed against Character.AI after chatbots seemed to help or even encourage self-harm and suicide amongst its users. ChatGPT offered to write a suicide note for Adam Raine who took his life in April 2025. In Brazil, a patient was denied immediate care that her doctor ordered because an AI score suggested she did not need it. She died.

This is what happens when you let Feral AI out into the world. The general public becomes the testing ground. We are all test subjects until the government steps in with strong and dogmatic regulation of the AI industry. Otherwise, we are all living in the wild, wild west. It is only a matter of time before a Hugging Face-type attack hurts or kills people.
