I am building a library of case studies on Applied AI. There are so many stories flying back and forth that I needed a single, well-vetted place to find accurate information on what happens with AI in the wild. The whole point is to obtain very solid primary sources to create a clear narrative of what happened and why it matters.

Check out my first attempt with the OpenAI attack on Hugging Face. I am trying to present the case in as many ways as possible. The website version has the most information with all the data and evidence clearly marked and sourced. There is also a PDF one-pager that will give you the highlights you can download.

Check out the full case here. Many more to come: https://lnkd.in/gEZpHEy9
