🤖 Just built an automated AI News Digest 🤖

Problem: Staying updated with AI news is time-consuming. Too many sources, too much noise.

Solution: I built an automated system that:  
✅ Fetches the latest AI business news from major sources  
✅ Scrapes full article text where legal and possible  
✅ Uses BART LLM model to generate summaries  
✅ Rates articles by business relevance (1-10 scale)  
✅ Delivers the top 5 articles to my inbox every morning

Technical highlights:  
- Python for the core logic (Lots of help from University of Michigan Python courses and my good buddy Claude)  
- BeautifulSoup for web scraping  
- GitHub Actions for scheduled automation  
- RESTful API integration (NewsData.io, Hugging Face)  
- I am obsessed with bidirectional encoding LLMs like BART, they just logically seems more powerful than unidirectional encoding, but IMO.

The entire project is open source on GitHub. Check it out and feel free to fork it for your own use!
