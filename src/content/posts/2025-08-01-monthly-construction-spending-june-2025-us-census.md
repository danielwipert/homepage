Monthly Construction Spending - June 2025 (US Census)

Construction Spending Slips as both Housing and Commercial Pull Back...

Topline: U.S. construction spending dipped in June, falling 0.4% month-over-month and down 2.9% from a year ago. This equates to 63 billion less spent on construction this June vs last June.

Residential: The downturn continues to be driven by weakness in residential construction, which fell 0.7% in June and is now down 6% year-over-year.

-- Both single family and multi family are down YoY. Single family is down 58 billion in construction spending YoY, multi is down 12 billion.

Nonresidential: A slight dip of -.1% MoM and -.5% YoY.

-- Interesting rise in Religious building construction. 17% increase YoY, up 660 million in construction spend.

-- Big aggregate movers are Commercial buildings (commerce, retail, and consumer-facing services) and Manufacturing (factories), together down 28 billion in construction spend YoY.
