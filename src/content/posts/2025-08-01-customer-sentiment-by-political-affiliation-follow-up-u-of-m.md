Customer Sentiment by Political Affiliation - Follow Up - (U of Michigan)

Dems and Indys in Despair, Republicans spew Hopium...

Today, University of Michigan updated their customer sentiment data broken down by political affiliation. I wrote a long piece last week on the historical data showing how politics determines economic sentiment long term (see link below)

The short story is that customer sentiment is continues to be intertwined and dependant on political affiliation, much more than salary, general geographics, or local business conditions. Political viewpoint plays a huge factor how people see the economy, perhaps it is the PRIMARY factor in how they view the economy.

The latest numbers confirm this pattern again. Dems are very pessimistic and scared about the future. Independents are not much happier or optimistic. Republicans think we are in Shangri-La. It is pretty mind blowing how political perspective changes how people view reality. Check out the latest numbers in my graph below.
