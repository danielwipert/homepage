I wanted to resurface this project I worked on earlier this year since it feels like everyone in AI is talking about loops and harnesses. For over two years now, I have been hyperfocused on creating AI systems that are usable and safe in the real world. For most of the year, I felt like I was swimming against the current. People wanted AI to do everything and be totally autonomous.

I will point to something like the frenzy over Open Claw. Anyone who has actually run a business or a department would laugh at the extreme naivety coming from the AI Utopians who embraced the radical freedom and zero oversight Open Claw embodied. Of course it was a catastrophic failure. Generative AI has not overwritten the core principles of business or technology. Yes, it is a huge leap forward and perhaps a new reality, but the Wright Brothers didn't change the laws of physics because they invented flight.

That is how I am approaching Applied AI Systems. Gen AI is a jet engine; now we need to build the plane around it. You strap a jet engine to a horse carriage, and you get Open Claw. This is why I built CASDAM, which is essentially a provable thesis as much as a working AI framework.

The concept is simple:

- LMMs will always hallucinate. Within their immense power lies an implicit willingness to deceive, which can never be uprooted. They are probabilistic systems, not reasoning systems. Always assume they will hallucinate.

- Because of the above. When building Applied AI Systems: Use multiple models. Use deterministic gates. Governance is the skeleton of all AI systems, not a wrapper.

That's it. Now, I went way deeper into these concepts in building CASDAM. But that is the entry point. Safe AI comes from safe systems, not safe models.
