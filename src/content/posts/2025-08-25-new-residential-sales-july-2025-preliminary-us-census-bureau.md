New Residential Sales - July 2025 Preliminary  (US Census Bureau)

New home sales slightly down, prices start to ease, supply still high...

--Good news--

- Prices are easing: Median (–5.9% YoY) and average (–5.0% YoY) are down; the share under $500k is higher than a year ago, signaling better affordability at the margin.

- Spec inventory available: Completed homes for sale are higher YoY (121k vs 103k), giving buyers more ready-to-move options

- West shows a MoM pickup despite remaining down YoY.

--Bad news--

- Sales pace weaker YoY: −8.2% vs last July (and YTD sales are –4% vs 2024).

- Inventory/supply still elevated: 9.2 months vs 7.9 a year ago; that’s consistent with a market still working through excess relative to demand.

- Time to sell completed homes is rising: 2.9 months since completion vs 2.2 last year—movement is slower.

--Neutral / mixed--

- MoM sales are essentially unchanged (–0.6%); Census’ 90% CI in your file is wide, so the MoM dip is not statistically significant.

- Inventory edged down slightly MoM (502k→499k), but composition shifted: fewer under-construction units, more not-started and completed, a mixed signal on pipeline vs immediate availability.

- Regional picture is uneven: West rebounded MoM, but YoY softness is concentrated in the West and Northeast.
