This is such a massive story, and it sounds similar to Barron's article that popped the dot.com bubble in March 2000.

Go read “Burning Up," published in Barron's on March 20, 2000, by Jack Willoughby. It sounds very similar to this article and the WSJ article that broke last night. We are starting to hear the word "Value" pop up a lot when talking about AI adoption. Back then, it was "Profit" that popped the bubble.

Here is what I think is happening. Classic group think and FOMO. AI pops up in late 2022 and blows away the entire world. It is insane what it can do. Then you add RAG into it, and AI hits a whole other level of crazy. We just had never seen anything like it.

Before anyone could properly investigate how AI would work in a business setting, Silicon Valley caught the AI Fever and then spread it throughout the business world. Everyone felt like if they didn't implement AI today, they would lose everything. This was the EXACT same ideology that happened with the dot-com boom and bust. It was all about timing and missing out; the train was leaving now; you must get on.

But here is the thing. The train isn't even built yet. That is what we have to do over the next decade. Build the systems and guardrails to contain the immense power of AI. Just like the last generation did with the World Wide Web.
