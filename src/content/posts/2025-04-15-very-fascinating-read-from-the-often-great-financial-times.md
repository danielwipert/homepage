Very fascinating read from the often great Financial Times.

“We didn’t believe him. We assumed that someone in the administration that had an economic background would tell him that global tariffs were a bad idea,” one Wall street executive says. “We are in for a roller-coaster ride.”

“Given our state of ignorance and all we don’t know, [investing now] is like betting on the outcome of the Super Bowl when you don’t know which teams are playing or who any of their players are,” Howard Marks, the co-founder of Oaktree Capital, says.
