The early read from the Consumer Sentiment survey is startling. It has come in at 50.8 -- the lowest ever recording for the survey, which has been running since 1952. The protectionist trade policy from the Trump administration is clearly the root cause here.

“This decline was, like the last month’s, pervasive and unanimous across age, income, education, geographic region and political affiliation,” Joanne Hsu, the survey’s director, said in a release.
