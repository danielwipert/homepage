Why AI models will always hallucinate...

One of the more bizarre things to witness over the last couple of years is the growing absurdity in the claims coming from AI's talking heads. This is especially true of leadership at the major AI companies. Their claims of superintelligence, artificial general intelligence, and decimated white collar work forces are totally irresponsible, wrong, and very poor business mgmt.

For anyone who has any background in AI, someone who actually understanding how it works, even on a basic level, the absurdity of the AI industry right now feels quite reminiscent of the early days of the world wide web. I am old enough to remember all the promises of the The Net back in the early to mid 90s. The internet would change everything, overnight. It did not. Now eventually it did, but that took decades. And while I do think AI will change things faster than the internet did, this change will take years and decades, not weeks and months. As the great Roy Amara said about technological change, "We tend to overestimate the effect of a technology in the short run and underestimate the effect in the long run."

How are we overestimating AI now? It's pretty simple. The industry is currently obsessed with models. Everything is about the new model, the new benchmark it hit, and how that model will take away millions of jobs. This obsession on models is extremely myopic for one simply reason. All gen AI models are inherently flawed. They will ALWAYS hallucinate, no matter how much we train them, ground them, or evolve them.

Gen AI, specifically LLMs, are probabilistic text generators. They are selecting each word in a sentence sequentially based on what it computes to be the most probably next word. That makes it incredibly flexible and trainable. But it also makes it inherently untrustworthy. Gen AI is probability, not reasoning, not truthful.

The solutions that are being put in place: Retrieval Augmented Generation and Constitutional AI are giant leaps forward. But they can not undo the foundational logic and technology that drives Generative AI. No amount of training, grounding, or prompt engineering will totally eliminate the inherent bias and hallucination implicit in ALL Gen AI models. The error rate for the best LLM on earth in a highly controlled environment with extreme guardrails is much higher than 1%. That makes a single LLM totally unusable for most business critical systems.

The solution to this is simple. We need to accept that bias and hallucination in Gen AI will never go away. So we need to engineering around it. We need to be focusing on building reliable, safe, and trustworthy AI systems, because we can not do that with a single model. That is why I am building Chorus AI Systems. I believe the future of AI is systems. Systems built with many different models. Systems built with the basic cybernetic principles in place: feedback loops and verification layers. This is how Gen AI will change the world.
