404 broke the story this week that consulting giant Accenture has put a hard cap on employee AI usage. Specifically, they are limiting employees to $29/month. We could all see this coming many months ago. It is disorienting how quickly corporate perspective, opinion, and language change.

Think back 6 months ago. Remember AI usage leader boards? Think back to 2025 when companies demanded their employees use AI for everything. Now here we are. Accenture actually seems quite late here, but I bet that has something to do with their exclusive deal with Anthropic. Other, much smarter companies, didn't tie their entire fleet to a single ship. Even AT&T wasn't that naive; they are aiming for 70% open-weight model usage by the end of the year.

At the very least, the first two chapters of my AI Operations Management textbook now probably make a lot more sense. AI is a utility, not software. AI needs to be managed by usage, not by seat, like SaaS. This Accenture story confirms that. They let their employees run wild, spending $500 or even $1000/month on AI. Without any clear and specific value add to the business, this cost is totally unjustified. Hence the cap and limit now.

Was Accenture paying API rates? That would be insane if true. If you have a company of tens of thousands, you need to diversify your AI offers outside of OpenAI and Anthropic. I am sure 99% of the work they were doing could have been done by Qwen and Deepseek, at 1/10th the cost at most. Accenture probably felt the need to jump headfirst into AI because it threatens the DNA of their business, but now they are seeing the downside of not properly planning or implementing AI tools. Ironic for a company that changes tso much money to tell other people how to use AI.

Here is how I see this playing out. AI tools need to be metered. The model and inference companies are absolutely metering; that's how you get the bill. But every company using AI needs to have their own meter. I will say Accenture made a good move here by requiring any costs above $29 to be attributed to a team. That's in my textbook! Attribution is foundational to AI Ops Management. AI is moving toward the metered token on both sides of the equation: provider and user.
