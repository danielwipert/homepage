Still having the internal debate about open weight models? The world has moved on; these tech leaders are making the leap into small language models and open-weight models. It is a vast ecosystem now, not just the big 3 of OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic:

"The world needs closed and open models. Firms and countries need to have control over their own infrastructure, and that requires open weights." - Jensen Huang, CEO of NVIDIA

"For 80% to 90% of enterprise use cases, you do not need a trillion-parameter model. What companies need is a 7-billion to 14-billion parameter model that is fast, cheap, predictable, and runs right next to their enterprise data." - Sridhar Ramaswamy, CEO of Snowflake

"When you look at production workloads at scale, companies realize they are paying a massive premium for general reasoning they don't actually need for a specific workflow. Open-weight models give engineering teams full control over fine-tuning, latency, and margin." - Tuhin Srivastava, CEO of Baseten

"Frontier models already have enough intelligence for 90% of the tasks we're trying to do. If open-weight models catch up, I may not need to use them anymore." - Vinay Kuruvila, Chief Technology Officer at Tinder

"At the scale of processing 45 billion tokens a day, cost is extremely important. If we can maintain accuracy, we will aggressively use far cheaper open models instead of expensive closed ones." - Andy Markus, Chief Data & AI Officer, AT&T
