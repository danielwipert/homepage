Monthly New Residential Construction - June 2025

The housing market in a state of hesitation: construction activity is holding steady overall, but completions are falling rapidly, which could worsen housing shortages if it persists. Builders are slowing down construction of single-family homes, while permits for multifamily buildings are rising. This likely reflects affordability challenges for buyers and growing demand for rental housing.

-- Good News --

- Housing Starts rose 4.6% from May, a sign of modest construction momentum.

- Multifamily permits (5+ units) increased 8.1% MoM, indicating potential strength in apartment demand. More rentals! 🏢

-- Bad News --

- 🚩Completions dropped sharply, down 14.7% from May and 24.1% YoY.  If completions stay low, it worsens the housing shortage, puts upward pressure on prices If this trend continues for another quarter, it could materially worsen the affordability crisis. 🚩

- Single-family permits fell 3.7% MoM and are down 8.4% YoY, suggesting caution among homebuilders.

- Single-family starts declined 4.6% from May, signaling possible softness in owner-occupied home demand.

-- Mixed News --

- Total building permits were essentially flat MoM (+0.2%) but down 4.4% YoY.

- While total starts are nearly level YoY (-0.5%), the multifamily segment showed solid YoY growth.
