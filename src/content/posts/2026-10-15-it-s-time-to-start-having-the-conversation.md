It's time to start having the conversation. Is there a Big 4 instead of a Big 3? My read on the AI market and industry is that three players dominate the field: OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google.

OpenAI is the consumer leader. They make AI simple for everyone, even if their agents are running amok across the internet. Anthropic is the best and enterprise-grade. Anthropic means quality. Google is the old-school leader with tentacles in nearly every company on the planet. You already have a relationship with them; just add AI for dessert. These are the Big 3, but is there actually a fourth?

DeepSeek is a Chinese company that releases open-weight models that rival the power and quality of the Big 3. The also vastly undercut them on pricing across the board. DeepSeek has never been spoken in the same breath as the Big 3. But based on OpenRouter usage for the last 6 months. Maybe it's time we expand the Royal Court of AI, time for the Big 4?
