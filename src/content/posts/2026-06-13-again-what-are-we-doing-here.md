Again, what are we doing here? Lawyers who charge $1,000 an hour making up cases in court in official court filings, now this...

Look, I have my own chip on my shoulder about the consulting industry. I have worked in startups for 17 years now. I have worked with every major consulting firm that exists. I often had to manage them. The results from them were, to be polite, inconsistent with a very nebulous ROI.

But this takes the cake. These so-called "experts" vibe wrote a case study on AI using AI and never checked any of the cited sources. These people get paid so much money. And this is what they produce.

The real lesson here is this, and I have been talking about this in every issue over the last two weeks of my newsletter Closing the Loop. Gen AI is probabilistic. Hallucination is baked into the DNA of the technology. It is never going away, no matter how many trillion parameters a model has. You need to build verification systems around LLMs. It is not that hard. Learn Python, or have AI build you a deterministic gate in Claude Code.

We are out of the "exploring" phase of Gen AI. We are now in the building systems phase. No one using AI for their job should ever trust the output from a single model ever; even the mythical power of Mythos is not powerful enough to not hallucinate. Build a moat around your LLMs; just make sure to have a drawbridge.
