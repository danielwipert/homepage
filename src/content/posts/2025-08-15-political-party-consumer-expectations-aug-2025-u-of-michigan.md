Political Party - Consumer Expectations- Aug 2025 (U of Michigan)

Highest gap EVER in Consumer Expectations between DEM and GOP...

University of Michigan has sent out an updated political affiliation data set for Consumer Sentiment.

They measure three different indexes here: Consumer Sentiment, Current Economic Conditions, and Index of Consumer Expectations.

I am really fascinated by the Index of Consumer Expectations, because it is a great barometer of how people feel about the economy, which will directly affect how they spend money.

The persistent gap we have seen between the three political groups surveyed (Dems, Indys, GOP) has now grown into historic levels of division. The Index of Customer Expectations in August 2025 for Dems is 29.6, for GOP it is 102.9. Just for some context, the Dem all-time average is 73, for GOP it is 78.

The Consumer Expectation gap betweens Dems and GOP is 73 points. This is the largest gap we have EVER seen in this survey going back to 1980. Absolutely insane. DEMs and GOP are simply not living in the same reality at all.
