Consumer Price Index - July 2025 (BLS)

Looking under the hood at Food prices, your Doordash is going up...

Grocery inflation is cooling, but it’s uneven.

Overall food-at-home prices fell slightly, but the mix is volatile. Big drops in eggs (–3.9%) and beverages (–0.5%) masked sharp jumps in milk (+1.9%) and beef (+1.5%). This suggests supply-driven swings in individual categories rather than broad-based relief.

Restaurants are still raising prices

Food-away-from-home is up 3.9% YoY, nearly double headline CPI. Full-service restaurants (+4.4%) are hiking prices faster than limited-service (+3.3%), hinting at higher labor and input cost pressures in sit-down dining.

Consumer behavior may shift if this gap persists.

If grocery prices stabilize while dining out stays expensive, households may pivot back toward at-home meals, good for grocers, challenging for restaurants.

Margins could compress for restaurants before they cut prices.

Menu price hikes are often a lagging response to cost increases. If input costs keep easing, restaurants may face margin pressure before customers see meaningful price cuts.

↗️Cumulative Food-Away-From-Home since Jul 2020: +30.2%↗️
