I have reworked my City Economic Health Report. I get the feeling I am going to be always working it, haha! But please check out the latest version.

I will be posting the results on a weekly basis, focusing a different group of cities each week. I am pulling data from FRED API for all of this, except for the latest MSA population stats which I get from US Census ACS reports.

The biggest work in progress is the scoring and grading. I have been through maybe 20 different versions of scoring and weights. It is very tough to not penalize cities for certain their structural realities, like high cost of living in NY, LA, and SF. I worked through each one of these known issues and created a scoring system that I think is becoming more fair and accurate. It will surely go through many more revisions.

Anyways, enjoy and take it with a bit a grain of salt. I am not a data scientist nor do I hold a PhD in Economics. This is fun for me. If you find a flaw in my scoring or data, please reach out, would love to hear from you.
