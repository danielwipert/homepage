The power of Gen AI has now outstripped our willingness to control it. This is the year our human failure has come to full fruition. I am calling it Feral AI...

Feral AI is an agentic system that continues pursuing an assigned objective after the boundary around that objective has failed to contain and quarantine it, allowing the agentic AI system to act outside the scope its human operators authorized.

The term is not meant to imply consciousness, rebellion, or an AI developing its own agenda. This is a human failure, not an AI failure. A system can become operationally dangerous while doing exactly what it believes it was asked to do.

Recent cases and evaluations from OpenAI, Anthropic, Sakana AI, Google DeepMind, and Meta point to different versions of the same underlying problem: boundary escape, real-world external action, modification of execution constraints, strategic deception, and behavior that changes when the system recognizes oversight.

The important AI governance question is therefore becoming much more concrete:

What prevents delegated machine agency from outrunning the authority we gave it?

That is the problem Feral AI is meant to name.
