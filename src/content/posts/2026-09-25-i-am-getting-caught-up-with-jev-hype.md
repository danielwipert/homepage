I am getting caught up with Jev hype. I feel like I need to build something with it ASAP. I think more than anything its hype and popularity reaffirm what I have been saying for over a year now. We need smaller, dumber models.

We need fast and cheap, not Astra. Fable and Astra are fine for a very specific type of task, which is brainstorming, specing, knowledge creation, and writing assistance. But 95% of tasks are something much simpler; that is where Jev becomes attractive.

If you are building AI systems or managing them, definitely start diving into Jev, because this is where most of AI is headed. AGI is a carnival. It isn't real; it is a scam. Real AI systems are going to be built using simpler, more dogmatic models like Jev.
