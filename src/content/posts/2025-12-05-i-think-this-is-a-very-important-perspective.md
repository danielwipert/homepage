I think this is a very important perspective. The bogeyman of AI is often overly hyperbolic. When we talk about AI, there is a group of people that use it as a shorthand for Artificial General Intelligence.

I personally think that leap to AGI is completely absurd and borderline irresponsible. To think that with the current AI technology in place, AI could reach human level comprehension, intelligence, and semantic improvisation just doesn't add up. Our collective lack of knowledge within the field of Philosophy of Mind is rearing its head here. We don't even understand human cognition. How exactly would we build AI to replicate it?
