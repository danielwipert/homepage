The AI jobs panic is getting a little overheated. The real story is less “robots stole my job” and more companies are reorging and AI is the newest excuse. Some jobs will change. Some tasks will disappear. Some productivity gains will be real. But the useful question is not whether AI replaces everyone. It is whether business leaders actually know where AI helps, where it doesn’t, and where they are just using it as a corporate fog machine.
