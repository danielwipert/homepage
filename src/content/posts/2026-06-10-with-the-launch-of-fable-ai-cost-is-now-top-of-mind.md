With the launch of Fable, AI cost is now top of mind. The All-You-Can-Eat era is over. It's all about Cost per Token. That is a game changer for Applied AI.

For anyone building agentic AI systems that have to work in production, cost is now a core decision driver. Two months ago, it wasn't

Fable has done two things. One, it has created a schism in the market between the haves and have-nots. That was already happening, but the outrageously high pricing for Fable has solidified that split.

You will have the Imperial models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. They will rule at the highest end of AI generation. But then you will have a vast ocean of Sovereign models that will be much cheaper, customizable, localized, and trainable.

Secondly, the launch of Fable has demonstrated that the one-model concept is fading. Fable costs way too much to run most agentic AI workflows. The big shift will lean towards smaller models that are cheaper and modular architecture for agentic AI workflows.

Throwing data at Fable is not an option for most businesses. You need a system, probably with many different LLMs, working in unison to get your desired output.

Cost is king, and AI system architecture is the new language we all need to learn asap.
