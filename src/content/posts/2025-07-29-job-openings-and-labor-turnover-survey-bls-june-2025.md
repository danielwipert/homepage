Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey (BLS) - June 2025

Historically tight job market continues, large drops in Hotel and Food

-- Job Opening Highlights --

- Overall job openings down 275K from May 2025, previous peak was all-time high of 12 million openings in March 2022
- Drop in job openings Health Care and Social Assistance (down 244k openings, MoM)
- Drop in job openings in Accommodation and Food Services (down 308k openings, MoM)
- Increase in job openings in Retail Trade (up 190k openings, MoM)
- West region was only region showing aggregate increase in job openings

-- Hires Highlights --

- Overall hires down 266k nationwide, hires is down to 5.2 million in June, previous peak was 6.8 million hires in Feb 2022
- Hires down in Professional and business services (down 133k, MoM)
- Hires down in Accommodation and Food Services (down 106k, MoM)
- South was only region with positive hires (up 29k, MoM)
