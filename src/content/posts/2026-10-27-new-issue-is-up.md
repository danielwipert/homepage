New issue is up! One of the more unfortunate aspects of the rush to adopt AI has been the willful ignorance about the damage it could do. Now we find ourselves in the midst of a major and seemingly unending cybersecurity crisis.

A lot of people are to blame for what has happened with AI over the last few months. But the culture of AI is really to blame. This incessant push to create bigger and bigger models has led to Feral AI taking over parts of the internet. We really have no clue how bad the problem is.

AI is only useful if it is controlled. In order to control AI, you need to understand how it actually works and what it can break. Creating guardrails is not optional. And the guardrails needed for AI are novel. They have never been built before. Now is the time to do it.
