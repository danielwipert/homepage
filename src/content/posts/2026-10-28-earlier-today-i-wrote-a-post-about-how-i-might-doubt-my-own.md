Earlier today I wrote a post about how I might doubt my own thesis about AI Operations Management. The crux of my argument, the core of my thesis, is that AI is a utility, not software. That is, AI should never be priced like SaaS because it is a variable cost that changes with the task it is being asked to complete. You don't sell AI, you sell inference and platforms that do the inference.

My doubt comes from the rapidly lowering cost of tokens. Maybe at some point, tokens from frontier models will be so cheap, you might as well price it like a piece of software, just a monthly subscription cost. Alas, enter OpenAI, who is making massive changes for their $200 pro subscribers, proving my entire thesis once again.

Taking effect on October 30, 2026, the restructuring targets the heaviest power users on the platform:

- Halved Capacity: The Pro $200 tier's allowance for flagship models drops from 20 times the base Plus tier down to 10 times. Weekly GPT-6 Pro limits have been slashed from 200 to 100 messages.

- The New Ceiling: To regain the previous 20x allowances and access "Ultrafast" processing, users must now upgrade to the newly introduced Pro 500 tier, priced at $500 per month.

- Strict Enforcement: Previously unenforced or generous rolling windows have been strictly clamped down to automated 5-hour rolling limits across all tiers.

This is the move many of us have been seeing for a year now. The salad days of getting $9000 worth of tokens for $200 a month are gone. This move underlines the entire economic model of AI. AI costs real *ucking money. It is GPU heavy, which is power heavy. The GPUs have a shelf life of what, 4 years? The physical reality of AI is being ignored for the moth flame of AGI. It is absurd and very dumb.

AI costs a lot of money. The subsidized reality of getting nearly endless inference for a low cost is coming to an end. The business model of AI is about inference and physical costs. The model wars are coming to an end. An off-the-shelf open weight from China can do what you need done for 1/10th the cost of American frontier labs. The cost and pricing will shift to the compute now: you need to pay for the GPU and power; the model is like free bread while you order.
