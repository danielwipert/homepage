Devs are leaving GitHub Copilot en masse this week. Why it matters to the entire AI ecosystem, perhaps the canary in the coalmine for AI costs...

Here is what happened: On June 1, 2026, Microsoft and GitHub completely overhauled how GitHub Copilot is priced. They transitioned the service from a predictable, flat-rate subscription to a usage-based billing model.

Ok so what? This is part of the paradigm shift in AI. The opening era of Gen AI was all about gaining adoption, getting users, as many as possible, as quickly as possible. To do this, companies offered their AI platforms at cutthroat pricing. It felt almost free.

Now we are getting the classic rug pull. A pillar of any capitalist system is bait-and-switch pricing. Get them in the door, get them using the product, then jack up the price. That is exactly what happened with GitHub Copilot this week.

Here is the immediate result of the "pricing strategy" change:

- One power user is saying their bill will go from $39 to $847 per month. A 22x cost increase.
- Multiple users on the $10 Copilot Pro tier reported burning through their entire monthly allotment in a single day of "light usage."
- An engineering team at Hindustan Times, which has over 150 developers, ran a preview of their June usage based on the new model. Their projected bill was roughly 9x higher than their flat-rate April spend.

Do some back-of-napkin math. If you are pushing AI into Agentic, you could easily have 85 million input tokens with maybe 1.2 million output tokens per month. You go from $39 a month to now $428 per month with the new pricing, 11x increase!!!

That might be a little edge case, but at the very least you are looking at 4x plus price increases for using GitHub Copilot. This is the change in the wind we are all sensing with AI that is happening right now. Token cost was ignored a month ago; now it is on the tip of everyone's tongue.
