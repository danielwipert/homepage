New York Fed’s Outlook‐at‐Risk from this week: Partly cloudy with a chance of rain

Outlook‑at‑Risk is a monthly risk dashboard that shows not just central forecasts, but how much things could deviate, especially if financial conditions worsen.

High Level Overview

- Demand environment is weak → Focus on efficiency, avoid aggressive expansion.

- Labor is still stable → Take advantage of steady employment to build bench strength.

- Price environment is soft → Lean on value, customer loyalty, and promotions, not premium pricing.

--Real GDP Growth (next 4 quarters)--

Percentile	Forecasted Annual Growth  
p10	                    -1.94% (very bad outcome)  
p25	                    -1.00%  
p50	                    -0.16% (median)  
p75	                    +0.64%  
p90	                    +1.44% (very good outcome)

- The median forecast is slightly negative (−0.16%), and there is nearly a 25% chance GDP could decline by 1% or more.

- Recession risk remains real, with more downside than upside.

- Plan for sluggish or negative growth.

- Avoid overextending on inventory, hiring, or CapEx unless you have clear demand signals.

- Most outcomes show falling or stable unemployment, with a small tail risk of rising joblessness.

- The 90th percentile suggests unemployment could rise modestly (~0.5%), but that is a less likely outcome.

- Labor markets are still resilient overall.

- Talent may remain competitive, so consider retention strategies and targeted hiring.

- Pricing power may weaken—don’t count on raising prices to protect margins.

- Input cost relief could be a tailwind, but watch for demand softening as well.
