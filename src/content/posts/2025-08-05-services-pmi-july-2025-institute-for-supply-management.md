Services PMI (July 2025, Institute for Supply Management)

Warning Signs Multiply in Services Sector: Backlogs Shrink, Jobs Fall, Prices Rise

Services PMI is a composite diffusion index that reflects survey responses from purchasing and supply executives across the U.S. service sector. A reading above 50 indicates expansion; below 50 signals contraction

- Services PMI: 50.1, down from 50.8 in June, barely in expansion territory, missing the forecast of 51.5

- Business Activity Index: 52.6%, still growing but cooled from 54.2 in June

- New Orders Index: 50.3%, lower than June’s 51.3%

- Employment Index: Dropped to 46.4%, marking contraction for the second consecutive month -- This reading indicates contraction in service-sector employment for the second consecutive month. -- This is the lowest level since early 2023 -- Services make up 70% of U.S. GDP and the majority of U.S. employment.

- Prices Index: Rose to 69.9% -- its highest since October 2022, with sustained readings above 60% signaling strong inflation pressures.

- Backlog of Orders Index: 44.3%, in contraction for the fifth month, reflecting reduced demand or delayed fulfillment. A clear warning signal. Firms are clearing orders faster than they’re receiving new ones. The trendline is steady contraction.

---Takeaways---

- Economic growth is fragile: The services sector barely eked out expansion, with soft new orders and contracting employment raising concerns

- Inflation remains persistent: Input price pressures remain elevated fueled by tariffs and rising commodity costs, raising stagflation risks. This also aligns with Regional Fed reports.
