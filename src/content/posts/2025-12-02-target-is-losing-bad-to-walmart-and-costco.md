Target is losing bad to Walmart and Costco. Can they pull out of their stall?

I wrote a long piece about Target's DEI Disaster a few months back. Their insane decision to reverse DEI policies in Jan 2025 to gain favor with second Trump admin will be a HBR case study within a few years. Textbook terrible decision. Target undermined its unique value proposition, which was their inclusive brand identity.

Target's 3rd quarter results were bad, but they were actually way worse than the headlines. Looking at comp sales, Target was down -2.7%. That includes the increase in sales from digital. In-store was down -3.8%. Compare that to Costco, who had comp sales rise 6.6% and Walmart who had comp sales rise 4.6%. Target is approaching death spiral territory now.

I joked that Target might go the way of Sears or Kmart if they don't make major changes asap. I was joking a few months ago. Now the joke isn't funny. Target is an existential crisis.

I do not buy the common reads on why Target is falling. Target is not failing due to pricing, product assortment, or omnichannel frictions. Target is failing because they destroyed their brand reputation. Target stood for inclusiveness. That inclusiveness is what made them unique. It intertwined with their "Chic Value" identity. That is the key point that retail analysts and Target themselves are missing.

Target being socially inclusiveness certainly helped them win more business from BIPOC and Queer communities. But what it also did is make them unique among value retailing. Their inclusiveness made them more attractive to people who are also inclusive. When they killed DEI, they not only upset the BIPOC and Queer communities, but they also upset their all of their allies. They upset people who believe inclusivity and acceptance of others is integral to a thriving community. That is a vast majority of their entire customer base.

Target basically insulted and attacked 80% of their customers by stopping DEI. No wonder they are in a tailspin hurtling towards the ground. The fix? Apologize profusely. Restart DEI 10x what it was. Hire a CEO who is not a straight white male. That is the only way out of this. There is no store remodeling, product assortment, or retail strategy that will save them. Their only hope is that they can regain the trust of the BIPOC, Queer, and Ally communities. Otherwise, they are toast.
