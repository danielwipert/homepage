This was always going to happen. AI is not going away, but the insanity needs to just stop. We are a decade away from the grandiose visions sold by even the most conservative AI prognosticators.

AI is not ready for primetime, and the corporations are clueless on how to use it properly. The mere fact they are paying for tokens? You know some of the best models are open weight? Why haven't you built your own systems? You have millions to invest but you haven't built your own hardware and software stacks to run LLMs? Pure insanity. I can't even with this short-sightedness. Let the crash happen, and let's move on to the next phase of AI development.
