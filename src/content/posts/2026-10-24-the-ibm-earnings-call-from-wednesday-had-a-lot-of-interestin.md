The IBM earnings call from Wednesday had a lot of interesting insights into how open-weight models are becoming the norm in enterprise. Arvind Krishna, the CEO of IBM, had lots to say about why open weight is becoming a real choice even for Fortune 100 companies. Here are some of the highlights:

- Open-weight models support private and sovereign deployment -- “run them on either on-premise infrastructure or on private clouds” -- Enterprises can control the environment in which the model, data, and operational logs reside.

- Data sovereignty is becoming commercially important -- Krishna said sovereign AI “is definitely becoming a much larger conversation,” especially for organizations that do not want proprietary data or usage logs exposed beyond their control -- This is not merely a compliance feature, IBM sees it as a demand driver for private AI infrastructure and open-weight models.

- The quality gap with frontier models is narrowing -- “90% as effective” -- Krishna says the gap has moved from a dramatic difference in late 2024 toward near-parity for many use cases.

- Lower cost can outweigh the remaining capability gap -- “10% of the cost”  -- If a non-frontier model handles a task nearly as well, the enterprise should not automatically pay frontier-model prices.

- Most work does not require the best model -- Krishna specifically cites summarizing email, examining contracts, answering customer calls, and composing email as tasks that cheaper models can perform well.  -- This is a workload-routing thesis: reserve expensive models for the relatively small share of tasks that genuinely require them

- Open-weight economics require real operational capability -- Krishna says enterprises still need people who understand GPU proximity, networking, clustering, and caching; otherwise the cost-performance advantage is difficult to realize -- The cheap-model advantage is not “download a model, save money.” It depends on infrastructure, inference engineering, and operating discipline.

As I said last week, the debate is over. Open weight is a vital part of most AI implementations and solutions. Unless you can make a convincing argument for paying 5x for the same AI power, you are going to be using open weight. OpenAI and Anthropic are luxury models, not work horses.
