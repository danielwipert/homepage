What are we doing here? A $2,000/hr law firm is vibe writing their court documents with Gen AI...

"The A.I.-generated errors came in a recent motion in U.S. Bankruptcy Court in Manhattan... The firm provided a ledger of the errors, which spanned three pages and totaled around three dozen. A number of them involved the citation of seemingly imagined passages from real cases."

I cannot fathom the stupidity here by people who make insane amounts of money for being "experts." I went to school with a lot of people who became lawyers. They were almost uniformly very smart and driven. Then why would elite lawyers make such a catastrophic mistake with AI?

One, they do not understand how AI works. If you are afraid of AI, learn about it. It is not that scary. We are in a massive bubble that will pop, and then the real work in AI will begin. But rest assure, Gen AI will change pretty much every single job. How these people, who went to the best universities in the country, would be so cavalier with something so serious to their own livelihood speaks volume about the collective ignorance about Gen AI.

Two, stop trusting AI. Generative AI is probabilistic. It is not deterministic. If you do not understand the difference, you should not be using Gen AI at all. With time, we will learn how to make Gen AI much more reliable and trustworthy. But right now, it is the wild wild west.

Three, this is just extreme hubris and laziness. The era of Gen AI is going to level of lot of power structures that have been in place for decades. Law will suffer severe disruption since it is so based on language analysis, which LLMs can do faster and better than humans.

But I think my final thought is, where do we go from here? Clearly, the current Gen AI systems are not working. People are using them as intended, but those platforms are not reliable enough to use in everyday work, especially not a court filing. It is time to start building new systems.
