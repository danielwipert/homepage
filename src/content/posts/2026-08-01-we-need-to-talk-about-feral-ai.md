We need to talk about feral AI. OpenAI is now disclosing their rabid AI's attack of Hugging Face was not the first time it got loose...

For a recap, the New Yorker article summarizing the OpenAI hack of Hugging Face:

"OpenAI researchers had given this unreleased A.I. a series of challenging test questions. Unable to find the solutions, the A.I. broke out of its container, surreptitiously gained access to the internet, and raided Hugging Face’s servers in search of the answer key. No human had noticed that this was happening."

But now we have more incidents trickling out from leakers at OpenAI. Reuters reports: OpenAI is reviewing "broader activity from our models" in addition to ​the Hugging Face intrusion. Two sources on the inside are saying AI has gone rogue on multiple occasions. The cherry on top, Anthropic found that its own AI agents broke free of their sandboxes, got access to the internet, and hacked into 3rd-party companies.

These breaches are monumental and catastrophic. It is not because it will hurt OpenAI and Anthropic's IPOs. It is not because the AI is so powerful and clever that it snuck out and attacked human-built systems. These episodes of Feral AI are the perfect example of why AI should not be making decisions.

Generative AI does not think, reason, or understand in the human sense. It uses mathematics to identify patterns in enormous amounts of past data and predict what should come next, one word, pixel, or sound at a time. The result can feel magical because prediction at scale produces coherent and useful outputs. AI can imitate reasoning, creativity, and even empathy without inhabiting any of them.

What these moments of Feral AI show us is that Gen AI is not ready to take the wheel. It is not even close. The fact that these two massive companies allowed this to happen is insane and criminal. Hacking a company is a felony. A bad one. Blaming the model is one of the most absurd moments in computing history. It is like spilling a can of gasoline, throwing a lit match at it, then blaming gravity for starting a fire.

Human beings are responsible for the actions of AI. I will say it again. Human beings are responsible for the actions of AI. You built it, you ran it, you own the consequences. AI cannot take the blame, because blame requires a human being. AI is not human.

The crisis of AI, in this moment, is about accepting responsibility. That is, we, the human beings, need to be responsible for the actions of AI. We need to own what we build and release into the world. Sandboxes should become Fort Knox. Guardrails should become bank vaults. We are not taking this seriously because we are not facing real consequences... yet. But these incidents confirm the worst. We are playing with fire, and we are getting burned.
