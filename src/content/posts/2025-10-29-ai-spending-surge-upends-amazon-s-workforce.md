AI Spending Surge Upends Amazon’s Workforce

“Artificial intelligence is not a feature anymore. It’s the foundation.”

The great AI Culling has begun en masse. It is hitting white collar and knowledge workers first. Anyone with any knowledge of Gen AI and with a deep work history in tech or startups knows what is coming.

Gen AI is not like any other technological change any of us has ever experienced. It will make the personal computer and internet look like a speed bump. Our entire economic foundation will be jackhammered, paved over, and rebuilt. This isn't even the beginning, this is the beginning of the beginning.

- Amazon is cutting 14,000 corporate jobs, its largest reduction since 2022

- Layoffs hit divisions across retail, devices, and human resources, underscoring how the company is rebalancing for the next era of computing

- Executives described the cuts as part of a broader effort to “focus investment where it matters most.”

- “We’re reshaping the company around intelligence itself,” one Amazon insider said.
