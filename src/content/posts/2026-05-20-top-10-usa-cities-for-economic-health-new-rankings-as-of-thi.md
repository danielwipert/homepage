Top 10 USA Cities for Economic Health: new rankings as of this week...

Raleigh reclaims the top spot with a stellar 3.3% unemployment number along with a rapidly expanding worker population.

Austin with an insane 7.8% increase in wages year over year. Just nuts.

Las Vegas is the odd one. Really high unemployment, yet its working population is increasing? No idea to be honest.

Also, good luck buying a house in the Hartford, CT area. The current median days on market is 25 days, with a year-over-year decrease of -16.7%.
