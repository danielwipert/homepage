Let's talk about the elephant in the room. Does AI actually add value? We are way past AI adoption, tokenmaxxing, and the appeal of open-weight models. We are now facing the much bigger question: does any of this even matter?

Keeping up with AI news is impossible. There are 100 stories every day that are important. Every day a new model, a new scandal, and a new way to have AI improve your life. It's all starting to feel like a satirical version of reality, like something you would see in GTA VI. But economic reality is hitting people like a shovel to the face right now.

Customer sentiment is in the gutter. The bottom 80% is being squeezed like it's Great Depression II. Corporations are scrambling to add AI while firing people. No hire, no fire was always a lie. They were always firing people with the hopes of replacing them with AI. Now AI has proven less than a miracle; corporations are pulling back on pouring 50% of their headcount budget into tokens. It's a mess, and AI is absolutely to blame for a lot of it.

But it is not really the technology of AI that is the source of the cacophony. The real issue is the distance between what the Silicon Valley set was promising vs. what AI can actually deliver. Right now, that distance feels like two sides of the Grand Canyon. You can see the other side, but you have no idea how to get there.

The question of AI value is going to be the defining question of the next decade. There is no single answer, and whatever answer you find will be highly subjective to the inputs and context of the question you are asking. But let's at least start building the bridge to the other side, where AI can actually deliver a positive economic gain. I am a firm believer that it can, but it will take a lot of hard work, human work, to make it happen.

First up, the massive study of how AI is assisting coders published an update in September. We can start there. How is AI helping software developers write more and better code? Is it? And if it is, how is that adding value downstream to the business? Or is it not?
