Why Gen AI is not ready for primetime...

According to internal documents obtained by Business Insider and statements from Amazon SVP Dave Treadwell, the company experienced a "trend of incidents" since the third quarter of 2025, including several major disruptions in recent weeks. At least one of these outages was directly tied to Amazon's AI coding assistant Q, highlighting the challenges of integrating generative AI tools into critical production systems.

Two specific outages had particularly severe consequences. On March 2, 2026, customers across Amazon marketplaces saw incorrect delivery times when adding items to their carts, resulting in nearly 120,000 lost orders and approximately 1.6 million website errors. An internal review identified Amazon's AI tool Q as one of the primary contributors to this incident. Three days later, on March 5, another outage caused a 99% drop in orders across Amazon's North American marketplaces, resulting in 6.3 million lost orders.

Amazon is going to put in some safeguards ("controlled friction") to help prevent these massive errors. They are also putting more Human In the Loop approval. They are accepting that Gen AI is not ready to be in full control over business critical decisions.

I am going to be banging the drum about this for at least the next decade. Gen AI is not deterministic, it is probabilistic. Gen AI will ALWAYS hallucinate because of the its technological and syntactical DNA. That is what makes it so flexible and powerful, but this comes at an inherent risk.

Gen AI will change the world. But it will not happen overnight. AI will only start to fulfill its immense promise when we start building human-designed systems that incorporate Gen AI, instead of use just letting Gen AI run wild. Right now, because of the massive pressure coming from the tech bros and investor class, companies are letting Gen AI take the wheel. This is a massive mistake.
