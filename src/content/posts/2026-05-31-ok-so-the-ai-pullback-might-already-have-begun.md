Ok so the AI Pullback might already have begun. Is the bubble popping, maybe? Here is a good breakdown from Reuters:

- The AI boom is now a macro story, not just a tech story -- Annual tech equipment and software investment has climbed past dot-com boom levels. That means AI spending is no longer just helping a few chip stocks. It is supporting earnings, market sentiment, and a decent chunk of the broader ecomonic growth narrative.

- The danger is not that AI turns out to be useless -- The more realistic risk is simpler; the spending pace slows. Markets are priced for hyperscalers to keep building data centers, buying chips, and racing each other into the future. They don’t need to stop. They just need to tap the brakes.

- If the AI trade slows, the damage does not stay contained -- Semiconductors and cloud names would take the first hit, but the shock could ripple into equity multiples, GDP expectations, and global capital flows. The “AI trade” has become big enough that a reversal would not feel like a niche tech correction.

My current take is that, yes, this house of cards is going to come down. It is simple financial physics. The market has run up AI like it is the Second Coming. AI is not there yet to show the returns to support this absurd level of optimistic investment. So it's going to come down, and it will not be contained to just tech.

I totally agree that is not The Emperor Has No Clothes moment. That also was not the case with the dot-com boom bust. But that is a moot point. Whether AI can deliver or not (it can), does not matter when so much overinvestment has taken place too fast.
