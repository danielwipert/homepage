Target Vs Walmart - YoY Revenue Growth

The recent CEO firing at Target made me want to take a closer look at their numbers. Like I say in most posts, I am not a financial analyst, but I have also helped build businesses for 15 years now. Most of the time, the basic numbers are all the only truth you need to see. Flowery language and Power BI won't change revenue or foot traffic.

Target has been stalling for a long time now, since Q2 2023. I had been following the Pride changes and then the DEI pullback, but I guess I wasn't aware of how poorly they were doing from a basic business perspective. We can throw out politics and the general economic situation. While those are affective inputs, they can not account for the clear downward direction in Target's sales. This is especially true when comparing to their rival Walmart, who has only gone up in the last 18 months.

The difference here has been stated as the Essentials of Walmart vs the Nice to Haves of Target. The more I have dug into that difference the murkier that explanation becomes. I think namely because Target is essentials for most of their customers. It certainly ain't luxury! I don't see a lot of people saying "Target is priced too high, I am going to Walmart." If value was that important, then you would already be shopping at Walmart. There is something else going on I think. Perhaps a topic for another deep dive...
