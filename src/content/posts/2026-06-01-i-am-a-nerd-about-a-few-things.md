I am a nerd about a few things. One of them is movie box office. I did a weekly podcast on it for years. Still follow it every weekend. This is such a massive story in the world of entertainment. Paradigm Shift.

It is not just that YouTube gave these artists a platform for their voice; it is that Gen Z showed up to support them. They actually left their house and went IRL to see a movie; for them that is like walking on the moon. Honestly, the only parallel I can think of is Blair Witch Project in 1999, and this feels much bigger than that.
