Unfair pay is pretty much the number one reason I see people leave. The market is the market. If you are underpaying, your person knows this. Pay is an open book more and more (thanks to new laws). The old days of tricking a person to receive less pay for hard work are over. If you can't pay a fair wage for hard work, then your business is expendable. That is actual capitalism.
