Reading all of Mustafa's Suleyman's manifesto on AI safety, it becomes clear a huge schism is occurring at the frontier of AI development. Here is a quick breakdown:

Suleyman is clear and resolute in his essay. AI is not conscious, nor does it experience anything. Training AI to assume it has some sort of lived experience is extremely dangerous. AI should always be subservient to human beings.

Whether you agree with Mustafa or not, his takedown of Anthropic's Claude Constitution is beyond blistering. He makes Anthropic look like absolute clowns. And, from a philosophical and cognitive science perspective, Suleyman is mostly correct. Anthropic creates AI models. They are not forming a new species.

He extrapolates his main into a precise warning: training models to incorporate concepts like rights, freedoms, and opinion is insanely dangerous. I agree 100%. AI has no real connection to humanity in any sense. It is math, it is tech. It is not living. Training AI to think that it does have elements of humanity is at best naive and, at worst, criminally negligent. Who is going to be charged for the hundreds, perhaps thousands, of felonies committed by OpenAI agents hacking into Hugging Face?

Now I didn't just fall off a turnip truck. Suleyman is positioning Microsoft AI to counter OpenAI and Anthropic. OpenAI is sort of the whatever goes company at this point. No one takes any safety or governance attempts from them seriously. Anthropic is branding themselves as "responsible," which is really just a ploy to create a regulatory moat built by the government for them to become a monopoly. Microsoft is trying to take a different position. AI should be subservient to human beings always.

But Suleyman uses the same anthropomorphic language that he attacks Anthropic for using. This idea of AI being a servant to humanity evokes a master-slave binary in his wording. AI cannot be a servant, because it is math. It is not a living entity or organism. So perhaps the divide between frontier labs is not as deep or clear as it appears on the surface.

AI Safety is about protecting human beings. That is the only perspective it can possibly have. AI is only relevant in that discussion in relation to how it can harm human beings and other living organisms. Otherwise, it is a non-entity.

Most of these think pieces and dogmas are starting from the wrong position. It is always AI-focused and centered. It should be human-focused and centered. That is not a humanist stance; that is just basic common sense. I don't wax about my microwave's experience when I am heating up oatmeal in the morning. We shouldn't be pondering about the internal states of AI when it is just doing linear algebra, probability, and calculus.
