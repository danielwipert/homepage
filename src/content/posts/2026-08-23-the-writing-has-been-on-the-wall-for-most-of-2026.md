The writing has been on the wall for most of 2026. Gen AI will be dominated by open-weight models, mostly from China. This is not to say OpenAI and Anthropic won't have their own massive hundred billion dollar lanes. They will.

The big US frontier labs will be the Iphone of Gen AI. They will have their very lucrative niche, but they will not dominate token usage. We will begin to see many more open-weight models pop up. How about the mysterious Ox Alpha that just appeared out of nowhere on Thursday? This will be the norm, many different models competing for your token usage at the lowest possible cost.

Anthropic and OpenAI would be absolutely stupid to try to play that game. They are platform and product companies, not model companies. So this news of Fable underperforming is not surprising nor should be alarming to Anthropic. They should be focusing on creating consumer-facing products and enterprise platforms with their world-class models as the foundation. They will never be able to undercut the Chinese offerings. Frontier is luxury; open-weight is the Toyota and Honda of Gen AI. They will win the most business by far.

"Spending on Fable 5, Anthropic’s largest and priciest model, has plateaued at only about 11 per cent of overall outlay on the company’s tools, more than two months after its release, according to spending data from 70,000 companies collected by payments group Ramp."

I think this also speaks to the myth of the magical model and AGI. Models are way past the point of being powerful enough to transform most business processes. We reached that point months ago. The increases in capability are not in line with actual usage. Enterprise agentic tools are so far behind where the models are, it will take years to catch up.

Why use Fable when you can use Opus? Why use Opus when you can use Sonnet? Why spend for Sonnet when you can get better for much cheaper on DeepSeek V4 Flash? Most business tasks just need a fraction of the power that Fable offers. Cost is gravity here.

And to hit home the point, just look at the model usage this week on Open Router, the premiere model routing company. No Fable. It doesn't take a weatherman to know which way the wind is blowing.
