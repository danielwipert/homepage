I love this approach. I think every major corporation will need to build their own LLMs and diffusion models based on their own company data.

The AI department coming to your company soon will need LLM experts who can train models based on your company's data. The reality is that Open AI and Anthropic will continue to battle for platform dominance. But the power and strength of AI is not just the platform. The power of AI is in the models and how models can be built into reliable systems.

I still think we are approaching AI incorrectly. The breakthrough and change will continue to come from the concept of Large Language Models and the transformer, which come from neural networks. You want to innovate in AI, you need to be there, at that level. Making your own models, training your own LLMs. The platform wars are a distraction. Get in the weeds.
