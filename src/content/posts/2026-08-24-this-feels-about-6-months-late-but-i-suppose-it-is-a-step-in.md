This feels about 6 months late, but I suppose it is a step in the right direction. Models are not systems; they are engines. But they are jet engines...

The big issue right now is that we don't know how to build the plane to harness these jet engines. Gen AI is not just powerful; it is way more powerful than anything we have seen before. It is also probabilistic. This means, unlike how core computer programming works, the output is extremely variable.

In technical terms, AI Engineering is probabilistic and non-deterministic. That stands in stark contrast to traditional software engineering, which is deterministic. We can definitely use the tools of software engineering to help build AI systems, but to say they are the same thing or similar would be incorrect.

The distinction can be illustrated easily. Traditional software engineering is like composing classical music. You write down every exact note, rest, dynamic marking, and tempo shift. The player follows it. Gen AI engineering is leading a jazz trio. You keep them in the same key signature, call out section changes, set the boundaries, and count off the tempo so they don't drift into complete noise. But you are not in full control. You are trying to affect their behavior, because total control is impossible.

These two types of engineering are simply not the same thing. But that being said, Trad Software engineering has decades of experience to help us build Gen AI systems. The harness concept highlights this. Here is how the old ways of doing things can help keep the dynamic and unpredictable nature of Gen AI models in line and working for us instead of against us:

- Deterministic Guardrails & Type Safety: Traditional software enforces strict schemas, regex filters, and compilers around probabilistic outputs to instantly catch hallucinations and ensure data integrity before touching downstream systems.

- State Management & Control Loops: Classical architecture provides the state machines, durable execution, and context management needed to keep stateless models on track, manage retries, and feed error traces back for self-correction.

- Observability & Reliability Engineering: Engineering practices like continuous evaluation pipelines (Evals), telemetry tracing, and circuit breakers translate chaotic model behaviors into measurable, production-grade reliability.

Yes, it is the time for harness, but that is the wrong word. The right wording is Applied AI Systems. Harness feels weak and cheesy. This is not some passing phase. All uses of AI will require guardrails, state management, and observability. That will be the ticket for admission to Applied AI Systems.
