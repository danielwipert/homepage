New York City Companies All but Stopped Hiring in H1 2025 - NY Times

This feels like clickbait, is it...

This article is huge on LinkedIn now, and I wanted to take a closer look at the data. The source here is data from NYC government,and its open for all to review. It was posted on July 18, 2025. It took NY Times a month to discover and write an article, interesting.

Diving into the spreadsheet, it does seem like this slowdown in hiring during the first half of 2025 is anomalous. The headline is that the NYC job market is cooling, especially in high paying jobs. But the nuance here is worth exploring.

- The first six month of 2025 has be an extraordinary slow period of job growth. This is not some statistical anomaly or noise. It is the slowest period of 6-month job growth since the depths of the Covid recession in 2020. That is a very bad sign, any way you look at it.

- On the other hand, when you smooth out the bumps and lumps in the data, the overall story is murkier. The YOY change is around a 58k increase in jobs. That is not great, but we have seen worse back in Oct/Nov 2023 when only 44k and 48k jobs were added for the trailing 12 months. So while the last 6 months has be very poor, the last 12 months is not the worst we have seen.

• But what are going to see in 6 months when we look back over the year? The reality is that a 6-month slowdown like this is quite severe. Before Covid, the last time we saw growth this low for a 6-month period was the Great Recession. Additionally, what signals are we getting that show any sort of positive turnaround. None. In fact, we are getting signals telling us it will get worse. ChatGPT said:  
Regional Fed surveys reveal that hiring has largely ground to a halt in most sectors..

-- The bad news is that this is really bad news. We are almost certainly in an Economic Stall. In a plane, a stall can be quiet peaceful and serene, even though you are falling out of the sky. The NYC job market is a leading indicator for the rest of the country.

What else can we say here? We are heading for a recession, or more likely we are in one now. The air speed warning is pinging loudly, and we don't have any more fuel to increase our thrust. We are gonna stall. The big question is how far we will fall.  --
