Ok so you want to start curtailing AI costs at your company. Pretty much every company is having to do this now. As we used to say in supply chain, expect what you inspect. The only way to control AI costs is to know exactly what they are.

Get your best data analyst or persnickety finance person to investigate AI cost. You need an audit, so find an auditor, either inside your company or outside. I used to manage fraud investigators at Etsy, any of them would be perfect for the job. You need someone who is obsessed with finding the truth. Find out what you are really spending, then start creating guardrails and limits. Here is a quick guide to reining in AI costs:

1️⃣ Implement Model Routing and Tiering

This is going to be the story of 2026 when we look back. The idea that you would use Astra or Fable for anything other than the hardest tasks will look insane. First, you need to be model and platform agnositic. Route most of your work to cheap open weight models like DeepSeek. Escalate tasks progressively up to higher models as their complexity increases. This and the last step are absolutely the hardest. So find the nerds at your company and give them a mission. They can probably make it happen.

2️⃣ Deploy Centralized AI Cost and Token Dashboards

Some companies offer this. But if you are building a routing system, you can just add this to the build. Tie AI cost to the user and the task, if possible. This is your meter; you should own it. It will become insanely important over the next 18 months.

3️⃣ Establish Clear Token Governance and Budget Caps

Once you have cost data and metrics in place, this one should not be too hard. If you can trace cost to a user, you can set a cap on that user, either soft or hard. One company literally just sends a slack message to a user who is reaching their cap. It says, do you want an increase today, yes or no. That alone stopped a ton of runaway AI costs. Put up guardrails made out of plastic or steel, your call.

4️⃣ Shift KPIs from Volume to Impact

This one is the leap across the Grand Canyon. This is also where AI stops being a tool and starts being a pillar of your business. If you measure usage and control usage, then the Final Boss is measuring value. I have no clue how YOU do that because it's your team and your business. You are the only one that can do this. But without it, AI is a pointless waste of time.

I would start simple and small. You have KPIs. Add a metric that compares your current KPIs with AI usage that could be linked to the KPI. Keep it general and high-level for now. You can refine it over time. But you need to show value right now, because the spend is right now.

The reality is that each layer here will be commodified and offered as a service. That is years away, though. You are spending on AI today. So it would behoove you to start building this out so that the VP of Finance doesn't come knocking on your door asking questions you can't answer.
